    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in Middletown

    Many older homes in Middletown still have sewer lines made from outdated materials like clay or cast iron, which are prone to cracking, corrosion, and root invasion. We offer upgrades to newer, more durable materials such as PVC or HDPE (high-density polyethylene). These materials resist corrosion, roots, and shifting soil, giving your sewer system a much longer lifespan.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Middletown, CT

    Traditional excavation is sometimes the best option, especially when the old line has a severe belly or the slope must be corrected. We dig along the line, remove the old pipe and set new PVC on compacted bedding at the proper grade. Rocky or hard-packed soil can slow digging, and we account for that in the estimate. Each joint is glued and checked before backfill. The inspector sees the open trench before we cover it.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Line Replacement in Middletown

    For major damage or certain pipe layouts, traditional trenching may be the most effective method. This involves digging a trench to access and replace the damaged sewer line directly. Our team carefully plans the excavation to minimize disruption to your landscape, using the right equipment for clean, precise digging. We make sure of proper grading and backfilling to prevent future issues and restore your property.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement in Middletown, CT

    To minimize disruption, trenchless sewer line replacement swaps out pipes without extensive digging. We offer pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward. This is ideal when protecting landscaping or driveways is a priority. The method works well for many homes in Middletown and can often be completed faster than traditional methods, using high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe known for its long life.

    Permitting and Code Compliance in Middletown, CT

    Sewer line replacement projects in Middletown, CT often require permits and must meet local plumbing codes. GLV Plumbing handles the permitting process and makes sure every part of the replacement meets or exceeds municipal standards. Our understanding of local regulations means your new sewer line is installed correctly and safely. That helps you avoid potential fines or future issues.

    Partial Sewer Line Repair in Middletown, CT

    Not every damaged sewer needs full replacement. If the camera shows a single break or one root-damaged joint, a spot repair may solve the problem. We locate the exact spot and depth, excavate a small area and replace that section. This costs much less than replacing the whole lateral. We only recommend it when the rest of the line is in good condition.

    Sewer Pipe Lining as an Alternative in Middletown

    Cured-in-place pipe lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. After cleaning, a resin-soaked liner is installed and cured in place. This works when the host pipe still holds its shape without major sags or collapse. Lining can be a smart option under slabs or finished areas. We tell you plainly if your line is a candidate.
