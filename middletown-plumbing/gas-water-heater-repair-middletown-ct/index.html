    Dependable Gas Water Heater Repair Services in Middletown, CT

    Pilot Light and Burner Issues in Middletown

    A common problem with gas water heaters is a pilot light that won't stay lit or a burner that fails to ignite. This can be caused by a faulty thermocouple, a dirty pilot tube or issues with the gas control valve. Our technicians in Middletown are skilled at diagnosing these components and performing the needed repairs, whether it is cleaning, adjusting or replacing parts. We ensure proper gas flow and ignition for consistent hot water.

    Venting and Draft Repair in Middletown, CT

    Atmospheric gas water heaters rely on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and confirm draft with a test. Proper venting is essential for your family's safety.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Middletown

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it detects vapors or overheating. Lint and dust in garages can clog the arrestor and trip the system. We clean the combustion chamber and replace sensors or thermal switches as required.

    Gas Valve and Thermostat Repair in Middletown, CT

    Issues with the gas valve or thermostat can lead to uneven water temperatures or a complete lack of hot water. Our team can test these key components for proper operation, checking for electrical continuity and gas flow. If a part is defective, we will replace it with a quality, manufacturer-approved component so your water heater heats efficiently and safely.

    Gas Water Heater Repair Cost in Middletown, CT

    Thermocouple and pilot repairs typically cost $150 to $350. Gas control valve replacement often runs $350 to $700, and power vent blower repairs can range from $400 to $800. A new gas tank installed usually costs $1,600 to $3,000 depending on size and venting. We help you decide based on tank age and condition.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Middletown

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures usually involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent. We read the error codes, test each part and repair the failure. Correct vent slope and termination are checked before we leave.

We also check the venting system for proper draft and obstructions, which is vital for safe operation and preventing carbon monoxide buildup in your Middletown home.
