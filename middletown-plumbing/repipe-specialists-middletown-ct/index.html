    Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Middletown, CT

    Homeowners on well water near Middletown should test regularly, since acidic water can cause blue-green stains and pinhole leaks in copper. Water in many areas contains minerals that contribute to scale buildup inside pipes over time. This hard water scale narrows the pipe's diameter, reduces water pressure, and can speed up corrosion, especially in galvanized and older copper pipes. Our repipe specialists consider local water conditions, recommending materials like PEX or properly installed copper that are more resistant to mineral buildup, ensuring a longer-lasting plumbing system.

    Copper Repiping in Middletown, CT

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes. Joints are soldered with lead-free solder or pressed with ProPress fittings. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a bit longer than PEX.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Middletown

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab. This is a good option for newer homes or a first-time leak. If the home has had multiple leaks, we will explain why a full repipe may save you money.

    Emergency Repipe and Leak Repair in Middletown

    While a full repipe is a planned project, sometimes a major pipe burst or multiple severe leaks can call for immediate action. Our repipe specialists in Middletown are equipped to handle emergency situations, providing temporary fixes and assessing the need for a more complete repiping solution. We can promptly isolate damaged sections and offer options for immediate repair or a phased repiping approach to restore your water service safely and efficiently, preventing extensive water damage.

    Drain Line Repiping in Middletown

    While often overlooked, your home's drain lines are just as important as your supply lines. Old cast iron or corroded drain pipes can lead to frequent clogs, slow drainage, and even sewage backups. GLV Plumbing provides complete drain line repiping in Middletown, replacing failing waste pipes with new, smooth PVC or ABS lines. This ensures efficient waste removal and prevents future drainage problems, protecting your home from foul odors and unsanitary conditions.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Middletown

    Kitec, with its orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe and brass fittings, and gray polybutylene pipe are both known for failures. Many homes built in the 1980s through early 2000s contain one or the other. We replace these systems completely so your home is easier to insure and sell. Visible pipe at the water heater often tells us which type you have.

    Efficient PEX Repiping in Middletown, CT

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very popular choice for repiping projects in Middletown, CT. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also highly resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a reliable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.

    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Middletown, CT

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average home often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper usually costing more. A single slab leak repair can run $1,000 to $4,000 including access and restoration. After two or three leaks, a repipe often pays for itself. We give you a side-by-side comparison so you can make the call.
