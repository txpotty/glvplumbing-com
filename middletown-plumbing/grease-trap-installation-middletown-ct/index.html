    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Middletown

    Hydro-mechanical grease interceptors, often called HGI units, are compact and designed for indoor use, usually under a sink or on the floor.     Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Installation in Middletown

    The cost of grease trap installation in Middletown, CT varies significantly based on the size, type, and complexity of the system.     GLV Plumbing provides a warranty on our workmanship for all grease trap installations. Additionally, the grease trap unit itself will usually come with a manufacturer's warranty. We will provide you with all warranty details at the time of installation, ensuring peace of mind.

    We calculate flow from every connected fixture, including sinks, floor sinks and dish machines. Hydromechanical units are typically rated by gallons per minute, while gravity interceptors are sized by volume and retention time.

    While the terms are often used interchangeably, 'grease trap' usually refers to smaller, indoor hydro-mechanical units, often found under sinks. 'Grease interceptor' usually refers to larger, outdoor gravity-fed units with higher capacities. Both serve the same purpose of separating FOG from wastewater.

    Grease trap installation is a complex commercial plumbing task that requires specialized knowledge, tools, and adherence to strict local codes. Attempting a DIY installation can lead to costly errors, code violations, and potential health hazards. It is always best to hire licensed and qualified plumbers like GLV Plumbing.

    The primary purpose of a grease trap is to intercept fats, oils, and greases (FOG) from commercial kitchen wastewater before they enter the public sewer system. This prevents pipe blockages, protects municipal infrastructure, and helps businesses comply with environmental regulations.

    Indoor traps should be close to the fixtures they serve and easy to open for cleaning. Outdoor interceptors are usually placed where a pump truck can reach them without blocking customer traffic.
