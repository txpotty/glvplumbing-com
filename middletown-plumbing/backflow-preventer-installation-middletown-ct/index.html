    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Middletown, CT

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Middletown, CT, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a popular and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers carefully install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also check for proper clearance and drainage.

    Commercial Backflow Prevention in Middletown, CT

    Commercial properties in Middletown, CT often have complex plumbing systems that pose a higher risk of cross-contamination and require robust backflow prevention. Restaurants, hospitals, industrial facilities and even multi-family buildings must comply with commercial plumbing codes. GLV Plumbing provides complete installation of heavy-duty backflow preventers, including large diameter RPZ assemblies, to safeguard public health and meet Connecticut regulations for businesses.     Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in Middletown, CT

    Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights. Frozen pipes are a real winter risk in Connecticut, especially lines in exterior walls, crawlspaces, and unheated porches or garages. We install insulated enclosures, freeze blankets or heated cabinets based on your local climate. Enclosures also discourage theft, since brass backflow assemblies are a common target for scrap thieves. We choose enclosures with enough room for testing and service.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Middletown

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a widely used backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Middletown, DCVAs are often installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers carefully install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Middletown, CT

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested. We carry trusted brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.
