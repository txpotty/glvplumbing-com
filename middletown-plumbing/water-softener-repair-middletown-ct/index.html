    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Middletown

    A frequent issue with salt-based water softeners is a salt bridge forming in the brine tank, preventing the salt from dissolving properly. This leads to poor regeneration and hard water. Our technicians are skilled at breaking up these bridges, cleaning the brine tank, and ensuring the float switch and brine well are functioning correctly. We also inspect for any leaks or cracks in the tank itself that could be compromising your system's performance, offering precise repairs or replacements.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Middletown

    If hard water symptoms return, the softener may not be regenerating. Causes include a failed timer motor, a bad circuit board, a stuck meter turbine or incorrect settings after a power outage. We test each component, replace the failed part and program the valve for your household size and hardness. You get soft water again after the next cycle.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Middletown, CT

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates suction to draw brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. When it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying. We clean or replace the injector and screen and check the brine line for leaks.

    Water Softener Leak Repair in Middletown, CT

    Leaks in a water softener can range from minor drips around fittings to significant puddles from a cracked tank or faulty valve. These leaks can cause water damage and waste water. Our plumbers quickly pinpoint the source of leaks, whether from seals, O-rings, hoses, or internal components, and perform durable repairs. We use appropriate sealing compounds and replacement parts for a lasting fix in Middletown, CT.
