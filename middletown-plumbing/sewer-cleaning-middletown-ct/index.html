    For the most stubborn sewer line clogs, hydro jetting is the top choice. GLV Plumbing uses professional hydro jetting equipment for sewer cleaning in Middletown, delivering a thorough cleaning that leaves your pipes nearly spotless, greatly reducing the chance of future blockages and extending the life of your sewer system.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Middletown, CT

    Many older homes have cast iron drains under the slab or floor. Aggressive cutters can break thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting made for older iron. This restores flow and gives a clear view on camera of the remaining wall.     Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Middletown, CT
