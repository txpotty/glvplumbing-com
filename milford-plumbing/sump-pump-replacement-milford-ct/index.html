    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Milford

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Milford, CT

    Some properties use pumps in yard drainage pits or below-grade window wells to handle storm runoff. These pumps may sit idle for months, then fail when they are needed most. We replace them with corrosion-resistant models and clear debris from the pit. We can route discharge away from foundations and onto approved areas. Regular testing before storm season is a smart habit.

    Sump Pump Basin and Pit Replacement in Milford, CT

    Sometimes it is not just the pump that needs replacing, since the sump basin or pit itself may be cracked, damaged or too small. GLV Plumbing offers sump pump basin and pit replacement in Milford, CT so your entire system works properly. A properly sized and intact pit is essential for the efficient operation and longevity of your new sump pump.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Milford, CT

    For added protection, many homeowners in Milford choose a combination sump pump system, which pairs a primary electric pump with a battery backup pump in one unit. This keeps the system running even if the power fails or the primary pump quits. GLV Plumbing can replace your existing combination system or install a new one, evaluating your needs for groundwater management. We make sure the components are properly matched for strong performance and longevity.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Milford

    Basement bathrooms and laundry rooms below the sewer line need sewage ejector pumps instead of sump pumps. These pumps handle solids and must be installed in sealed, vented basins. We replace ejector pumps with models rated for two inch solids. We also check the basin seal, vent and discharge check valve. Proper installation stops sewer gas odors.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Milford

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Milford for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit. When replacing a submersible pump, we carefully remove the old unit, clean the pit and install a new, appropriately sized pump.     Key signs include the pump not turning on, unusual noises such as grinding or rattling, running nonstop, visible rust or corrosion, or an age over 7 to 10 years. Any of these could point to coming failure and a need for replacement.
