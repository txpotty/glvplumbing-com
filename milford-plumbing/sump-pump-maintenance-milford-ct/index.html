    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Milford, CT

    The float switch is a key component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to ensure reliable operation for your home in Milford, CT.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Milford

    Heavy downpours can overload older sewer systems and push water back into basements, so a backwater valve can be a sensible upgrade. We test your pump ahead of the rainy season by filling the pit until the float trips. We watch the pump empty the basin, listen for grinding bearings and measure how fast it cycles. If the pump struggles, you still have time to fix it before the first big storm.

    Emergency Sump Pump Maintenance in Milford

    If your sump pump is acting up or has stopped working, GLV Plumbing provides emergency maintenance services in Milford. We focus on rapid response to diagnose and address urgent issues, whether it's a jammed impeller, a faulty float switch or a complete pump failure. Our goal is to promptly restore your sump pump's function to prevent immediate flooding and protect your property from water damage.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Milford

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to early wear. We confirm these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your Milford residence.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Milford, CT

    A power outage during a heavy storm can leave your primary sump pump useless. Our maintenance service includes complete testing of your battery backup sump pump system. We check the battery charge, verify the charger's function and confirm the backup pump activates when the main power is interrupted. This dual protection is vital for homes in Milford, CT where power interruptions can coincide with severe weather.

    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Milford, CT

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil near the slab or foundation.

    Zoeller and Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Milford

    GLV Plumbing is proficient in maintaining all major sump pump brands, including popular models from Zoeller and Wayne. Our technicians understand the specific components and maintenance requirements for these trusted brands, so your unit receives expert care. Whether it's a submersible Zoeller pump or a pedestal Wayne pump, we provide thorough service to keep it running well for your Milford home.

    Annual Sump Pump Check-ups in Milford

    Regular annual maintenance is crucial for the longevity and reliability of your sump pump. Our technicians perform a thorough inspection, checking the motor, impeller, float switch and discharge lines for any signs of wear or blockage.
