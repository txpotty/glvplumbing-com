    While often overlooked, your home's drain lines are just as important as your supply lines.     Efficient PEX Repiping in Milford, CT

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very popular choice for repiping projects in Milford, CT. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also very resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use high-quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a dependable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.
