    Commercial Pit Pump Replacement in Bridgeport

    Commercial buildings often have elevator pits, parking garage drains and mechanical room sumps. These require pumps rated for higher flow and sometimes oil-sensing controls.     Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Bridgeport

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Bridgeport

    Power outages during severe storms can leave a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump offers important protection during these times. We install and replace complete battery backup systems, including dedicated marine-grade batteries and smart charging units. Our team helps you select a system with enough run-time to protect your Bridgeport home through extended power interruptions. That gives you peace of mind when the lights go out.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, powered by your home's electricity.
