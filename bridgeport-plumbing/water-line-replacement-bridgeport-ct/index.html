    PEX Water Line Installation in Bridgeport, CT

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a modern, flexible, and very popular choice for water line replacement. It resists corrosion, scaling, and chlorine, making it very durable for most water conditions. Outdoor faucets should be shut off from inside and drained each fall, and frost-free hose bibs are a worthwhile upgrade. PEX can expand and contract without bursting, which is a significant advantage when a cold snap hits Bridgeport. Its flexibility allows for fewer fittings and quicker installation.

    Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Bridgeport

    Galvanized steel pipe was common in homes built before the 1970s. Over time, it corrodes inside and chokes the pipe. This causes low pressure and rusty water. We replace galvanized lines with modern PEX or copper. The difference in water pressure is often immediate.
