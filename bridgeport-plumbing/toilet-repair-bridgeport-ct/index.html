    Leaking Toilet Repair in Bridgeport

    Toilet leaks can cause significant water damage to your bathroom floor, subfloor, and even the ceiling below. Leaks can originate from the base of the toilet, the tank, or the supply line. Common culprits include a worn-out wax ring, loose bolts, or cracked porcelain. We thoroughly inspect all potential leak points, replacing seals, tightening connections, or recommending bowl replacement if the porcelain is damaged, preventing further water intrusion.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Bridgeport, CT

    A hairline crack in the tank or a bulging braided supply line can let go without warning and flood a bathroom. We inspect the tank, tank-to-bowl bolts and gasket, and the supply line during every visit. If the porcelain is cracked we will price a replacement tank or new toilet, and we upgrade old plastic or vinyl supply lines to braided stainless.

    Clogged Toilet Solutions in Bridgeport, CT

    Stubborn toilet clogs are a common nuisance in Bridgeport, CT homes, often caused by non-flushable items or excessive toilet paper. While a plunger can clear minor blockages, persistent clogs indicate a deeper issue in the trap, drain line, or even the main sewer line. Our plumbers utilize professional-grade augers and hydro-jetting equipment to safely and effectively clear even the toughest clogs without damaging your toilet or pipes. We also inspect for potential causes like root intrusion or pipe corrosion, which are sometimes seen in older homes.

    Toilet Tank and Bowl Crack Repair in Bridgeport

    Cracks in a toilet tank or bowl in Bridgeport can lead to significant water leakage and require immediate attention. Our technicians assess the severity of the crack and advise on the most cost-effective and durable solution.     Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Bridgeport, CT

Some low-flow toilets from the 1990s simply flush poorly, and we will tell you plainly if replacement is the better fix.
