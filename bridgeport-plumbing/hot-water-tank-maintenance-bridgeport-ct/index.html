    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Bridgeport

    A properly working temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to confirm it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will recommend immediate replacement to keep your Bridgeport home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Bridgeport

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause loud popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution rated for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Emergency Hot Water Tank Inspections in Bridgeport

    If you notice sudden changes in your hot water tank's performance, like strange noises, discolored water or a sudden drop in hot water supply, GLV Plumbing offers emergency inspections in Bridgeport. While not a full maintenance, we can promptly diagnose the issue and advise on immediate steps. That usually includes a focused maintenance procedure to restore function and prevent further damage.

    Electric Element and Thermostat Service in Bridgeport

    Electric tanks rely on upper and lower heating elements that get crusted with scale in hard water. We test each element with a meter, check the thermostats and look for scorched wires at the terminal block. If an element has failed we replace it with the matching wattage and voltage. Many families who think they need a new heater just need a lower element and a flush.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Bridgeport, CT

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can clog the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a stable blue flame and lower gas bills.

    Standard Hot Water Tank Tune-Up in Bridgeport

    GLV Plumbing offers a complete tune-up for conventional hot water tanks, focusing on critical components. This includes draining the tank to remove sediment buildup, which is crucial wherever water carries minerals, and inspecting the anode rod for signs of corrosion. We also check heating elements, thermostat settings and all pipe connections for leaks. Our goal is to optimize your tank's performance and prevent future issues.
