    Faulty Shower Valve Repair in Bridgeport

    Problems with your shower's water temperature or pressure often point to a malfunctioning mixing valve or cartridge. These components control the hot and cold water flow. Our technicians are skilled in diagnosing valve issues and can repair or replace them, ensuring you get consistent water temperature and pressure every time you shower. We work with all major valve makers.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Bridgeport, CT

    Leaks under the shower pan or behind tile can cause rot, mold and stains on ceilings below. We use moisture meters and flood tests to find failed grout, cracked pans or leaking pipe joints. Then we repair the source, whether that means resealing a drain, replacing a valve or rebuilding a pan. On slab homes, these leaks can damage flooring in adjoining rooms.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Bridgeport, CT

    Weak shower pressure is often caused by mineral buildup in the showerhead or flow restrictor. It can also come from a clogged cartridge or a failing pressure regulator. We clean or replace the showerhead, check the cartridge and test whole-home pressure if needed. Most low-pressure issues are fixed fast.

    Showerhead and Arm Repair in Bridgeport

    Dripping showerheads, leaking arm connections and broken handheld hoses are common problems. We clean or replace showerheads, reseal shower arms and install new handheld hoses and holders. We can also add a slide bar for a handheld shower. Hard water clogs nozzles, so we recommend heads with flexible rubber nozzles that are easy to clean.

    Clogged Shower Drain Solutions in Bridgeport, CT

    Slow or standing water in your shower is a clear sign of a clogged drain. These clogs are usually caused by a buildup of hair, soap scum, and sometimes mineral deposits. Our team uses professional drain snakes and hydro-jetting equipment to safely and effectively clear even the most stubborn blockages without damaging your pipes. We also offer advice on preventative measures to keep your shower drains flowing freely.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Bridgeport

    If you are experiencing inconsistent water temperature, low water pressure, or difficulty turning your shower on or off, the shower valve may be failing. A faulty mixing valve can lead to scalding or freezing water, which is a safety hazard. We can replace old or broken shower valves with new, dependable models, including pressure-balancing and thermostatic options for improved safety and comfort. This often involves reaching the valve behind the shower wall.

    Shower Drain Repair in Bridgeport

    Slow shower drains are usually clogged with hair and soap scum, often hardened by mineral deposits. We clear the drain with augers and check the drain assembly for leaks. Loose or cracked drain bodies can let water seep under the pan. We repair or replace drain components as needed.

    Leaking Shower Valve Repair in Bridgeport

    A shower that drips after it is turned off usually has a worn cartridge or seals. Hard water scale scratches cartridge surfaces and breaks down O-rings faster than soft water does. We replace the cartridge with a genuine part and lubricate the valve with silicone grease. This stops the drip and makes the handle turn easily again.
