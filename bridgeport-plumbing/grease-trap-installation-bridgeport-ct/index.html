    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Installation in Bridgeport

    For high-volume commercial kitchens in Bridgeport, large outdoor grease interceptors are often required by code. These substantial units are installed underground and designed to manage significant amounts of FOG before wastewater enters the municipal sewer system. We handle the whole process, including excavation, precise placement, connection, and final inspection, ensuring a robust and compliant installation.

    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Bridgeport

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with limited space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid easily.

    Grease Trap Sizing and Placement in Bridgeport, CT

    Correct sizing and smart placement are critical for a grease trap's effectiveness. Installing a unit that is too small will lead to frequent clogs and overflow, while an oversized one can be an unnecessary expense. Our plumbers carefully calculate the required capacity based on your kitchen's water flow, fixture count, and operating hours, delivering optimal performance for your Bridgeport, CT business.

    Grease Trap Installation Cost in Bridgeport, CT

    Small under-sink hydromechanical traps generally cost much less to install than large outdoor interceptors. Indoor units are usually a few thousand dollars installed, while in-ground interceptors can run well into five figures with excavation and concrete work. Pricing depends on unit size, location, soil conditions and whether we need to cut and patch floors. We give you a written estimate that includes permits and inspection. That way there are no surprises during construction.

    New Restaurant Build-Out Grease Traps in Bridgeport

    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Installation in Bridgeport

    The cost of grease trap installation in Bridgeport, CT varies significantly based on the size, type, and complexity of the system. Small under-sink units might range from $1,000 to $3,000, while large outdoor interceptors requiring excavation can cost anywhere from $5,000 to $20,000 or more. We provide clear, upfront pricing after a thorough on-site assessment.

    The primary purpose of a grease trap is to intercept fats, oils, and greases (FOG) from commercial kitchen wastewater before they enter the public sewer system. This prevents pipe blockages, protects municipal infrastructure, and helps businesses comply with environmental regulations.

    We calculate flow from every connected fixture, including sinks, floor sinks and dish machines. Hydromechanical units are usually rated by gallons per minute, while gravity interceptors are sized by volume and retention time.

    We install all common types of grease traps, including gravity grease interceptors, hydro-mechanical grease traps (automatic grease removal units), and compact under-sink grease traps. Our team helps you choose the best system for your commercial kitchen's specific needs and local code requirements.

    Installation time depends on the size and complexity of the grease trap. A small under-sink unit might take a few hours, while a large outdoor interceptor could require several days due to excavation and concrete work. We strive to complete installations efficiently to minimize disruption to your business operations.

    Most food service businesses that cook, wash dishes or prepare food with fats, oils or grease are required to have one. Local pretreatment programs set the specific rules, and we can help you find out what applies to your site.

    We install a variety of reputable grease trap brands, including Zurn, Big Dipper, Schier, and Rockford Separators. Our plumbers are familiar with the specifications and installation requirements of numerous manufacturers, making sure you get a high-quality, durable system tailored to your needs.
