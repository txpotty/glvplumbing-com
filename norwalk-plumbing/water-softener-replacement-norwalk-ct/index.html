    Metered Softener Replacement in Norwalk

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly exhausted. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first few weeks.

    Replacing Pelican Water Softeners in Norwalk

    Pelican Water Systems are popular for their whole-house filtration and softening solutions, including both salt-based and salt-free options. If your Pelican system in Norwalk is nearing the end of its lifespan or needs a major upgrade, our technicians are experienced in their replacement. We make sure your new Pelican system or an equivalent quality brand is installed to factory specifications for strong performance and warranty validity.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Norwalk, CT

    For homeowners in Norwalk, CT looking for an alternative to traditional salt-based systems, salt-free water conditioners offer a different approach. These units, sometimes called descalers, condition water to prevent mineral scale without removing the minerals or adding salt. While they do not produce soft feeling water, they reliably protect pipes and appliances from scale. We can replace your existing salt-free system or help you switch from a salt-based unit.

    Water Softener Failure Diagnosis in Norwalk, CT

    Before replacing, we check whether your softener has a simple fix like a clogged injector, failed motor or wrong settings. If the valve is obsolete or parts are no longer available, replacement is typically the better value. We test hardness before and after the softener to confirm the problem.     Dual Tank Softener Systems in Norwalk

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water around the clock. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand. They cost more upfront but avoid hard water bleed-through during regeneration.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Norwalk, CT

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more economical than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes more sense.

    Traditional Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Norwalk

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and effective solution for hard water in Norwalk. These systems use ion exchange to remove calcium and magnesium minerals, which are well known for causing scale buildup. We replace old, inefficient salt-based units with modern, high-efficiency models that use less salt and water. Our plumbers confirm proper brine tank installation and regeneration settings for strong performance.
