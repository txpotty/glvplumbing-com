    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Installation in Norwalk

    The cost for garbage disposal installation in Norwalk, CT usually ranges from $200 to $500, not including the unit itself.     A 1/3 or 1/2 HP disposal suits light use, while 3/4 to 1 HP units are a better fit for families and frequent cooking.

    Most disposals carry manufacturer warranties from two to ten years, and we stand behind our installation labor.

    While some homeowners attempt DIY installation, it's usually recommended to hire a professional. Improper installation can lead to leaks, electrical hazards, and damage to your plumbing system. Our licensed plumbers provide a safe, code-compliant, and reliable installation.

    To prolong the life of your new garbage disposal, avoid putting hard items like bones, fruit pits, or fibrous vegetables down the drain. Always use plenty of cold water while operating the disposal and run it regularly, even if there's no waste, to prevent rust and keep components moving.

    Common signs you need a new garbage disposal include frequent clogs, loud grinding noises, leaks from the unit, a persistent foul odor, or if the unit simply won't turn on despite troubleshooting. These usually indicate motor failure or irreparable damage, making replacement the best option.
