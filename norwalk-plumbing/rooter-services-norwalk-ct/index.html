    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Norwalk

    Rooting a sink, tub or toilet typically costs $125 to $300. Main sewer line rooting through a cleanout generally runs $200 to $500. Adding hydro jetting brings the total to about $450 to $1,000.

    Tree roots are naturally drawn to water and nutrients, which they find in sewer lines through tiny cracks or loose joints. As roots grow, they can expand and completely block the pipe, a common issue in Norwalk neighborhoods with mature trees and older pipes.

    Homes with tree roots or older clay pipe often need rooting every 12 to 24 months. Homes with newer PVC lines may go years without it. A camera inspection can help set the right schedule.

    Rooting is safe when done by trained technicians using the correct cable and blade. Badly cracked or collapsed pipe can be harmed by any cleaning method. We watch for warning signs and stop if the pipe shows damage.

    Rooting cuts roots back but does not seal the pipe joints, so roots will eventually regrow. Root treatments slow regrowth, and pipe lining or replacement provides a permanent fix.

    Snaking uses an auger to break through and pull out blockages, while hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the entire inside of the pipe, removing buildup like grease and mineral deposits. Hydro-jetting provides a far more thorough cleaning, often recommended for recurring clogs or main sewer lines.
