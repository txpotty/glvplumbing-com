    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Norwalk, CT

    Water around the base typically means the wax ring has failed or the toilet has been rocking on a loose flange. On slab foundations, the flange often sits on concrete and can crack when bolts are overtightened. We pull the toilet, inspect the flange, install a repair ring or new flange if needed and reset the bowl on a fresh seal. We also check for soft flooring so you know if any water damage needs attention.

    Running Toilet Repair in Norwalk

    A running toilet is not just annoying, it can waste hundreds of gallons of water a day, leading to much higher water bills. This common issue is usually caused by a faulty flapper, a misaligned fill valve, or an improperly adjusted float. Our plumbers promptly identify the exact cause, whether it's mineral buildup from hard water affecting the flapper seal or a worn-out component, and replace the necessary parts to stop the continuous flow and restore efficiency.

    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Norwalk, CT

    The gaskets and seals within your toilet tank and at its base are essential for preventing leaks. Over time, these rubber components can degrade, crack, or become brittle due to age and exposure to water, especially where the water is hard. We focus on replacing worn-out flapper seals, tank-to-bowl gaskets, and the wax ring at the toilet's base, which is essential for preventing leaks onto your bathroom floor and maintaining a watertight seal.

    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Norwalk, CT

    If your toilet needs two flushes or clogs often, the problem may be scale in the rim jets, a partial blockage in the trapway or a flush valve that does not open fully. We auger the trap, check the vent if gurgling is present and clean the jets with a descaling treatment. Some low-flow toilets from the 1990s simply flush poorly, and we will tell you honestly if replacement is the better fix.

    Toilet Flush Mechanism Repair in Norwalk, CT

    If your toilet isn't flushing properly, it could be due to issues with the flush chain, handle, flapper, or fill valve. A weak flush may mean the tank isn't filling correctly, while a loose handle might not be engaging the flapper. Our plumbers are skilled at diagnosing these internal tank problems, replacing worn or broken components like flush valves, fill valves, and toilet handles to restore full and effective flushing power.

    Clogged Toilet Clearing in Norwalk, CT

    Stubborn toilet clogs are a frequent problem that can bring your bathroom to a halt.
