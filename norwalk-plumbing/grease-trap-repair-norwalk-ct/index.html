    Dependable Grease Trap Repair Services in Norwalk, CT

    Damaged Baffle Repair in Norwalk

    The baffles inside a grease trap are critical for separating FOGs from wastewater. If these baffles are corroded, broken, or dislodged, the trap's efficiency plummets, allowing grease into the sewer system. Our service includes inspecting the condition of all baffles and repairing or replacing them as needed, often using durable, corrosion-resistant materials. Proper baffle function is key to compliance with wastewater regulations in Norwalk.

    Commercial Grease Interceptor Repair in Norwalk, CT

    Larger commercial establishments often utilize grease interceptors, which are more substantial than standard grease traps. These systems require specialized knowledge for repair, often involving more complex piping and larger capacity tanks. Our technicians are trained in the repair of all types and sizes of commercial grease interceptors, making sure your high-volume kitchen in Norwalk, CT remains compliant and functional, regardless of the system's complexity.

    Flow Control Device Repair in Norwalk

    Hydromechanical traps rely on a flow control fitting to keep water moving at the rated speed. If this fitting is missing or clogged, the trap can be overwhelmed and grease escapes. We inspect, clean or replace flow control devices during repair visits. We also check the air intake that allows the device to work properly. Correct flow control is necessary for a trap to pass inspection.

    Grease Line Clog Repair in Norwalk, CT

    Grease that hardens in the lines before or after the trap can cause backups even when the trap itself is clean. We use hydro jetting to clear the pipe and remove buildup. A camera inspection helps us confirm the line is clear and check for damage. If we find broken or sagging pipe, we can repair the section. This keeps water and waste moving smoothly.

    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Norwalk, CT

    Tees direct flow into and out of the trap at the correct depth. Missing or broken tees allow floating grease to escape into the sewer line. We replace damaged tees with durable PVC or other approved materials. We also check that they are set at the right depth for your unit. This is one of the most common and necessary grease trap repairs.

    Grease Trap Repair vs Replacement Cost in Norwalk, CT

    Simple repairs like gasket or tee replacements usually cost far less than a new trap. Major damage such as a cracked tank body, failing concrete or a badly corroded steel unit may make replacement the better value. We compare repair cost, remaining life and compliance risk for you. Older steel traps often rust through and are good candidates for upgrade. We give you options and pricing in writing so you can decide.

    Grease Trap Odor Control & Repair in Norwalk

    Persistent foul odors emanating from your grease trap are a sign of inefficiency, excessive FOG accumulation, or bacterial growth. Beyond cleaning, our repair services address the root cause, whether it's poor ventilation, sludge buildup, or structural issues preventing proper flow. We make sure all components are working correctly to contain and process waste effectively, eliminating unpleasant smells that can drive away customers in Norwalk.
