    Jet Pump System Repair in Norwalk, CT

    Jet pumps, usually found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Common problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. GLV Plumbing can reliably troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Norwalk operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is essential for jet pump function.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Norwalk, CT

    Many well pump issues stem from electrical problems, including tripped circuit breakers, faulty wiring, or a malfunctioning control box. These can prevent the pump from starting or cause it to run erratically. Our licensed plumbers are also skilled in electrical diagnostics, safely testing circuits, relays, and capacitors to identify and repair electrical faults. We ensure all wiring and connections meet Connecticut electrical codes, providing a safe and reliable power supply to your well pump.

    Diagnosing Low Water Pressure in Norwalk

    Low water pressure from a well system can be a frustrating problem, often indicating issues beyond just the pump itself. This could be due to a failing pressure switch, a partially clogged well screen, a leak in the supply line, or even a pump that is no longer meeting demand. Our team conducts thorough diagnostics to pinpoint the exact cause of low pressure in your Norwalk home, ensuring a targeted and effective repair solution.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Norwalk

    Submersible pumps are widely used in residential wells, designed to operate submerged within the well casing. When these pumps fail, symptoms often include no water, low water pressure, or strange noises from the well. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like motor burnout, damaged impellers, or faulty check valves that can affect submersible pump performance. We use dedicated equipment for safe and efficient pump extraction and reinstallation, ensuring the integrity of your well system.
