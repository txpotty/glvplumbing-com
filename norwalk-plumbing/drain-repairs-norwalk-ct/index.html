    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Norwalk

    Simple trap or slip-joint repairs typically cost $150 to $350. Replacing a section of drain pipe usually runs $400 to $1,200, and cast iron stack replacement can range from $2,000 to $6,000 depending on access. We provide a written price before starting.

    Minor clogs can sometimes be resolved with plungers or enzymatic drain cleaners. However, for stubborn blockages, recurring issues or suspected pipe damage, it is best to call a professional. DIY attempts with harsh chemical cleaners can sometimes damage pipes or hide a larger problem.

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to clear out stubborn blockages, grease buildup, mineral deposits and even tree roots from your pipes. It is commonly used for severe clogs or as a preventive measure to thoroughly clean drain lines, leaving them almost like new.

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of pipes, thoroughly removing stubborn clogs, grease buildup and even tree roots. It's typically used when traditional snaking isn't enough, or for preventive maintenance on main sewer lines, especially in older homes with buildup issues.

    Drain lines can break or collapse due to several factors, including shifting soil, tree root intrusion, corrosion from age, extreme temperature changes or heavy loads passing over underground pipes. Poor installation or defective materials can also lead to early failure.

    Common signs include slow draining sinks or tubs, gurgling noises from drains or toilets, unpleasant odors coming from your drains, recurring clogs or water backing up into fixtures. If you notice any of these, it is smart to call a professional before the problem worsens.
