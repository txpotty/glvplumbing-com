    Tank Leaks & Pressure Problems in Norwalk

    Even gas water heaters can develop tank leaks, often due to corrosion from hard water or high water pressure. Homeowners on well water near Norwalk should test regularly, since acidic water can cause blue-green stains and pinhole leaks in copper. While some leaks may call for a replacement, minor leaks or pressure relief valve issues can often be repaired. Our team checks the extent of the damage, inspects the anode rod and addresses pressure imbalances to prevent further problems in your Norwalk home.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Norwalk, CT

    A yellow, lazy or noisy flame signals a dirty burner or poor combustion air. We remove and clean the burner, check the orifice size for natural gas or propane and adjust as needed. A clean blue flame heats more quickly and produces less carbon monoxide. We also check for rust flakes falling from the flue.

    Propane Water Heater Repair in Norwalk

    Rural properties often run water heaters on propane instead of natural gas. Propane units use different orifices and regulator pressures, and improper conversions cause sooting and poor performance. We confirm the correct parts are installed and repair pilots, valves and burners. We also check tank regulators for correct pressure.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Norwalk, CT

    The gas control valve regulates temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something goes wrong. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or listed universal model. We leak test and confirm temperature control afterward.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Norwalk

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it detects vapors or overheating. Lint and dust in garages can clog the arrestor and shut down the system. We clean the combustion chamber and replace sensors or thermal switches as required.

    Pilot Light and Ignition System Repair in Norwalk

    A frequent culprit for no hot water in gas heaters is a faulty pilot light or ignition system. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like a dirty pilot tube, thermocouple failure or a malfunctioning igniter. We carry common replacement parts on our service vehicles to ensure a quick fix and restore your hot water flow promptly.

    Venting and Draft Repair in Norwalk, CT

    Atmospheric gas water heaters rely on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and confirm draft with a test. Proper venting is critical for your family's safety.

    Water Heater Tank Leaks and Pressure Issues in Norwalk, CT

    While gas water heaters are sturdy, tank leaks or pressure problems can occur. We can often fix small fitting leaks or replace relief valves if pressure is too high. If the tank itself is compromised due to rust or old age, a replacement might be the more sensible long-term solution. We will provide an honest assessment of the repair options for your Norwalk, CT home.
