    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Norwalk

    The cost of well pump replacement in Norwalk, CT usually ranges from $1,500 to $3,500, though it can vary. Factors influencing the price include the type of pump, well depth, horsepower and any additional components needed like pressure tanks or wiring upgrades. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after a thorough assessment.

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up from shallower wells, usually less than 25 feet. Submersible pumps are placed directly inside the well casing, submerged in water, and are used for deeper wells, pushing water up to the surface. Each has specific applications and benefits.

    The lifespan of a well pump varies greatly depending on usage, water quality and pump type, but most last between 10 and 20 years. Regular maintenance and proper sizing can extend its life, but eventually wear and tear will call for replacement.

    If your pressure tank is old or waterlogged, replacing it at the same time can protect the new pump. A failed tank causes rapid short cycling, which is one of the most frequent reasons submersible motors burn out early.

    New well pumps usually come with a manufacturer's warranty, usually ranging from 1 to 5 years, covering defects in materials and workmanship. GLV Plumbing also backs our installation work with our own labor warranty. We will discuss warranty details for your chosen pump during the quoting process.

    Low pressure, sputtering faucets, no water and high electric bills can signal pump trouble. Frequent cycling is another warning sign.

    It is often a good idea to consider replacing your pressure tank at the same time as your well pump, especially if both are of similar age. A new pressure tank can improve the efficiency and lifespan of your new pump by reducing cycling. It also cuts labor costs to do both jobs at once.

    Common signs of a failing well pump include no water, sputtering faucets, reduced water pressure, discolored water or unusually high electricity bills. If your pump runs constantly or cycles on and off too frequently, these are also signs that replacement may be necessary.
