    Pool, Outdoor Equipment and Water Heater Line Freeze Damage in West Haven, CT

    Pool equipment lines, outdoor kitchen and ice maker supply tubing and water heater connections in garages are all common freeze points.     Hose Bib and Outdoor Faucet Repair in West Haven, CT

    Outdoor faucets freeze quickly, especially with a hose left connected. A split often hides inside the wall and only shows when the faucet is turned on in spring. We replace damaged hose bibs with frost-free models from brands like Woodford and Prier that shut off water inside the warm part of the wall. Insulated covers add more protection on freezing nights.

    Preventative Pipe Winterization in West Haven

    Prevention is key to avoiding frozen pipes in West Haven. We offer thorough winterization services, especially for homes that might be vacant or have vulnerable plumbing. This includes draining irrigation systems, insulating exposed pipes in attics or crawl spaces, and advising on proper faucet dripping during cold snaps.     Frozen Pipe Thawing in West Haven

    When a faucet only trickles or nothing comes out after a cold night, ice is blocking the line somewhere upstream. We open a faucet downstream to relieve pressure, then locate the frozen section with thermal imaging. Copper and galvanized pipes can be thawed with an electric pipe thawing machine that warms the metal from the inside. PEX and CPVC are warmed slowly with heat guns or infrared heaters to avoid softening the plastic.

    Burst Pipe Repair in West Haven, CT

    Water expands as it freezes, and that pressure can split copper, crack PVC fittings and pop PEX connections. Leaks usually appear only after the ice melts, sometimes hours later. We shut off the water, open the wall or ceiling only where needed and replace the damaged section. Every repair is pressure tested so you know the line is solid.
