    Navien Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in West Haven

    Navien tankless water heaters often feature condensing technology, making them highly efficient but also requiring specific maintenance. Our tune-ups include checking the condensate trap for blockages, cleaning the secondary heat exchanger and verifying the operation of the circulator pump. We also ensure a proper air-fuel mixture and calibrate sensors for peak efficiency in West Haven homes.

    Condensing Unit and Neutralizer Service in West Haven

    High-efficiency condensing tankless heaters produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized before it goes down a drain. We inspect the condensate trap, line and neutralizer cartridge and replace the media when it is spent. A clogged trap can cause shutdowns or water leaking inside the cabinet. We also check that the line is shielded where it runs outdoors.

    Commercial Tankless System Checks in West Haven, CT

    Commercial tankless water heaters face higher demand and require more frequent, rigorous maintenance. Our service for businesses in West Haven includes detailed inspections of multiple units, checking for proper gas pressure, water flow rates and exhaust venting. We look for any signs of wear on components like igniters, sensors and heat exchangers, which is crucial for businesses that rely on consistent hot water, such as restaurants or salons.

    Isolation Valve Kit Installation in West Haven, CT

    Some tankless heaters were installed without service valves, which makes proper flushing very difficult. We install a set of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines so future maintenance takes minutes. The kit also lets you shut off water to the heater without closing the main. It is a one-time upgrade that pays for itself in simpler service.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Flushing in West Haven

    A large share of homes in Connecticut use private wells, which can be acidic or carry iron, manganese, or radon that call for treatment. Hard water can lead to significant mineral buildup in tankless water heaters. Our residential flushing service in West Haven involves circulating a descaling solution through your unit's heat exchanger to remove calcium and magnesium deposits. This process brings back efficiency, prevents overheating and extends the life of your appliance, so your family gets continuous hot water without interruption.

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Descaling in West Haven, CT

    Noritz tankless heaters are known for their robust build. Our descaling service for Noritz units focuses on their intricate heat exchangers, which can be prone to scale buildup from mineral-rich water. We use manufacturer-approved descaling agents and follow precise flushing procedures to prevent damage. We also inspect the fan motor and exhaust system for proper airflow, a common area for attention in West Haven.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in West Haven

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system thoroughly. Restored flow means more stable temperatures and fewer shutdowns.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in West Haven

    Most tankless descaling visits cost $175 to $350, depending on brand and access. Adding an isolation valve kit usually runs $150 to $300 more.

    While some basic tasks like cleaning external filters might be possible for a homeowner, complete maintenance, especially descaling the heat exchanger and inspecting gas lines, should be left to a licensed professional. Improper servicing can damage the unit, void warranties and create safety risks related to gas and carbon monoxide.

    The descaling process involves shutting off the unit, isolating the water lines and connecting a circulating pump to flush a specialized acidic solution through the heat exchanger.     Common signs include reduced hot water flow, fluctuating water temperatures, strange noises like banging or popping, error codes displayed on the unit, or a noticeable increase in your energy bills.
