    Frequently Asked Questions About Clogged Drain Repair in West Haven

    Clearing a single sink, tub or shower drain usually costs $125 to $300. Harder clogs that need a larger machine or access through a roof vent run $250 to $450. Hydro jetting a heavily scaled or greasy line typically costs $350 to $800.

    While chemical drain cleaners are readily available, we generally advise against them. They often contain harsh chemicals that can corrode pipes, especially older ones, and can be harmful to your health and the environment. Professional plumbers use safer, more effective methods.

    Drain snaking uses a flexible auger to physically break through or pull out blockages. Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of pipes, thoroughly removing scale, grease, and roots, providing a much more complete cleaning. Hydro-jetting is often used for tough clogs or preventative maintenance.

    A plunger or a hand-held hair snake can handle many simple tub and sink clogs. If the clog returns, affects more than one fixture or does not respond to a plunger, call a plumber. Forcing a cable can crack old pipes or scratch fixtures.

    We use professional-grade equipment from reputable manufacturers known for durability and effectiveness. This includes drain snakes and augers from brands like RIDGID, high-definition sewer cameras for inspection, and powerful hydro-jetting machines designed for various pipe sizes and clog types.

    Common signs of a clogged drain include slow drainage, gurgling sounds from drains or toilets, unpleasant odors coming from your pipes, and water backing up into sinks, tubs, or showers. If multiple drains are affected, it often indicates a main line issue.

    Almost never. We access drains through the fixture, trap, cleanout or roof vent. Opening a wall is only needed if a pipe is broken and must be swapped out.
