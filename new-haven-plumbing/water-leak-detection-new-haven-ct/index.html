    Irrigation Leak Detection in New Haven

    Drip and sprinkler systems run underground across yards and often account for hidden water loss. Cracked tubing, broken emitters and stuck valves can waste huge amounts of water. We isolate the irrigation system, test each zone and trace leaking lines. Repairs restore efficient watering for your landscape.

    Leak Detection for PEX and Copper Pipes in New Haven

    Whether your plumbing system uses modern PEX tubing or traditional copper pipes, GLV Plumbing has the expertise to detect leaks in both materials. PEX, while flexible, can be vulnerable to damage from rodents or improper installation, while copper can corrode over time, especially with hard water. Our tools are effective across various pipe materials, ensuring accurate detection regardless of your home's plumbing type.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in New Haven

    Leaks in underground water lines, from your meter to your home, can go unnoticed for long periods, wasting water and causing yard erosion. Signs include oddly green patches in your lawn, persistent wet spots or a constantly running water meter. GLV Plumbing employs ground-penetrating radar and tracer gas detection methods to accurately map out and pinpoint leaks in buried pipes, allowing for targeted excavation and repair rather than digging up your entire yard.

    Hidden Wall & Ceiling Leak Detection in New Haven, CT

    Leaks behind walls or in ceilings can cause significant damage before they become visible, leading to stained drywall, peeling paint or a musty odor. These often stem from faulty pipe connections, old galvanized pipes or issues with shower pans and drains. Our technicians use moisture meters and infrared cameras to scan these areas, identifying elevated moisture levels and temperature differences that point to a hidden leak, minimizing the need for extensive wall demolition.

    Water Meter Leak Testing in New Haven

    The water meter is the first and simplest leak test. With every fixture and appliance off, a moving leak indicator means water is escaping somewhere. We read the flow rate to estimate how many gallons are being lost. Then we shut off zones one at a time to see which part of the property is leaking.

    Main Service Line Leak Detection in New Haven, CT

    The line from the meter to your house carries full pressure and can leak underground for a long time. Wet soil, soft spots or oddly green patches of yard may be the only clues. We trace the line, listen for the leak and mark it for a targeted repair. Replacing old galvanized or polybutylene service lines is usually recommended.

    Pool Water Leak Detection in New Haven, CT

    Pools lose a lot of water to evaporation in summer heat, so a leak can hide behind normal loss. We use a bucket test to compare evaporation with actual water loss. Then we pressure test pool plumbing and dye test fittings, lights and skimmers. Finding the leak safeguards the pool and the soil around it.

    Slab Leak Detection in New Haven

    Slab leaks are common in homes on shifting soil and moving foundations. These leaks occur in pipes beneath your concrete slab, often signaled by warm spots on the floor, sudden spikes in your water bill or the sound of running water when all fixtures are off. GLV Plumbing uses dedicated acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to precisely locate these hidden leaks without destructive guesswork. Early detection is essential to preventing major foundation damage.
