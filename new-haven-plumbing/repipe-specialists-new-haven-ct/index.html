    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in New Haven

    Copper piping offers exceptional durability, corrosion resistance, and a long lifespan, making it a premium choice for many homeowners in New Haven. While the upfront cost is higher than PEX, copper provides superior rigidity and resistance to UV light if exposed. Our team meticulously solders each joint, creating a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that can withstand the test of time, including changing temperatures and local water conditions. Copper also naturally inhibits bacterial growth, contributing to cleaner water.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in New Haven

    Kitec, with its orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe and brass fittings, and gray polybutylene pipe are both known for failures. Many homes built in the 1980s through early 2000s contain one or the other. We replace these systems completely so your home is easier to insure and sell later. Visible pipe at the water heater often tells us which type you have.

    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in New Haven, CT

    Copper Repiping in New Haven, CT

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes. Joints are soldered with lead-free solder or pressed with ProPress fittings. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a bit longer than PEX.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in New Haven, CT

    Polybutylene pipes, installed in many homes between the 1970s and 1990s, are notorious for premature failure due to chemical reactions with disinfectants in the water supply. If your home in New Haven, CT has polybutylene, a full replacement is essential to prevent major water damage. GLV Plumbing offers specialized polybutylene plumbing replacement, carefully removing the old, brittle pipes and installing a reliable new system.

    Efficient PEX Repiping in New Haven, CT

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very popular choice for repiping projects in New Haven, CT. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also highly resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a reliable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.
