    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in New Haven

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in New Haven

    The cost of a sump pump replacement in New Haven, CT can range from $500 to $1,500, depending on the type of pump, its horsepower and the complexity of the installation. Factors like needing a new sump pit, major re-piping or adding a battery backup system can influence the final price. We provide transparent, upfront quotes after assessing your needs.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, powered by your home's electricity. A backup sump pump, usually battery-powered or water-powered, is a secondary system that activates if the primary pump fails, loses power or cannot keep up with heavy water inflow. Many New Haven homes are better off with a backup system due to storm risks.

    We install a variety of proven sump pump brands known for their reliability and performance, including Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty Pumps and Little Giant. Our plumbers can help you choose the best brand and model for your home's requirements and budget.

    Sump pumps generally have a lifespan of 7 to 10 years. Even if they appear to be working, their efficiency can decrease over time. Regular inspection and testing are crucial, and replacement is advisable as they approach the end of their expected service life.

    Most sump pump replacements take one to two hours. Battery backups or new discharge lines take a bit longer.

    GLV Plumbing provides warranty coverage on both the sump pump unit itself, per the manufacturer's warranty, and our workmanship. We stand behind our installations for your peace of mind and a reliable, long-lasting solution.

    Most standard sump pump replacements can be finished within 2 to 4 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new pit excavation or major discharge line work, might take longer, up to a full day.

    While it may seem like a simple task, sump pump replacement involves electrical connections, proper plumbing and making sure the pump is correctly sized and installed to prevent future flooding. DIY replacement can lead to improper operation, electrical hazards or system failure. It is best to hire a professional plumber for a safe and effective installation.
