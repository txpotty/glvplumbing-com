    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in New Haven

    Direct vent water heaters draw combustion air from outside and vent exhaust through a sealed system. They are a good choice for tight utility rooms or interior closets.     Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in New Haven

    Atmospheric vent water heaters rely on natural draft to move exhaust through a metal flue. They are common in older homes and are often the most affordable gas option. We inspect the existing vent for damage, proper slope and clearance. If the vent is undersized or corroded, we replace it. Proper draft is critical for safety.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Installation in New Haven

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, strange noises like rumbling or popping from the tank, or visible leaks around the base of the unit. Older units, particularly those over 10 to 12 years old, are also candidates for replacement.

    Rusty water, leaks, pilot problems and slow recovery are common signs. If your heater is over 10 years old, replacement may be the smarter option.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for gas water heater installation. We pull permits when required and ensure the work passes inspection.
