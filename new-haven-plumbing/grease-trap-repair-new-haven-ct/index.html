    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in New Haven

    When traditional snaking isn't enough, hydro-jetting is our go-to method for severe grease trap blockages. This powerful technique uses high-pressure water streams to thoroughly scour the interior of pipes and the grease trap itself, removing years of FOG buildup, scale, and debris. It's a very effective, non-invasive method that not only clears clogs but also cleans the lines, greatly reducing the likelihood of future blockages in your New Haven business.

    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in New Haven, CT

    Tees direct flow into and out of the trap at the correct depth. Missing or broken tees allow floating grease to escape into the sewer line. We replace damaged tees with durable PVC or other approved materials. We also check that they are set at the right depth for your unit. This is one of the most common and important grease trap repairs.

    Grease Trap Odor Control & Repair in New Haven

    Persistent foul odors coming from your grease trap are a sign of inefficiency, excessive FOG accumulation, or bacterial growth. Beyond cleaning, our repair services address the root cause, whether it's poor ventilation, sludge buildup, or structural issues preventing proper flow. We ensure all components are working correctly to contain and process waste effectively, eliminating unpleasant smells that can deter customers in New Haven.

    Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in New Haven, CT

    The internal baffles of a grease trap are crucial for separating FOG from wastewater. If these are damaged or dislodged, the trap becomes inefficient, allowing grease to escape into the sewer system. We repair or replace broken baffles and cracked lids in New Haven, CT, making sure your trap functions as designed. A secure lid also prevents odors and keeps pests out, maintaining hygiene around the unit.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Repair in New Haven

    The cost of grease trap repair in New Haven varies widely depending on the nature and severity of the problem.     While minor clogs might seem DIY-fixable, grease trap repair is generally best left to licensed plumbers. These systems involve complex plumbing, potential health hazards from wastewater, and strict local codes in Connecticut. Improper repairs can lead to greater damage, environmental fines, or health code violations for your establishment.

    A broken grease trap can release large amounts of FOGs into the municipal sewer system, leading to blockages and sanitary sewer overflows. These overflows can contaminate local waterways, harm aquatic life, and spread bacteria, posing significant environmental and public health risks in New Haven, CT. Proper repair and maintenance are essential for environmental protection.

    Grease traps are smaller, usually located inside or near the kitchen, and are suitable for low-volume establishments. Grease interceptors are much larger, often installed underground outside the building, and are designed to handle the high FOG volumes from large restaurants and commercial facilities. Both serve the same purpose of preventing FOGs from entering the sewer system.

    Our plumbers are experienced in repairing all major brands and types of commercial grease traps and interceptors, including but not limited to Zurn, Big Dipper, Schier, Proceptor, and Rockford Separators. We have access to parts and expertise for a wide range of models common in New Haven, CT.

    Most standard grease trap repairs, such as clearing a blockage or replacing a baffle, can be completed within a few hours. More complex issues, like repairing a leaking tank or extensive pipe damage, may take a full day or require multiple visits. We always work to limit disruption to your business in New Haven.

    Staff can replace a lid gasket on some small traps, but most repairs require plumbing knowledge and proper waste handling. Professional repair makes sure the trap meets code.
