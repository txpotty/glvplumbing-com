    Resin Bed Cleaning and Regeneration Optimization in New Haven, CT

    The resin beads are responsible for ion exchange, removing hard minerals from your water. Over time, these beads can become fouled with iron or other contaminants, reducing their capacity. Our maintenance includes cleaning the resin bed and adjusting the regeneration cycle settings for maximum softening capacity. We confirm your system is regenerating efficiently for your water quality, providing truly soft water.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Inspection in New Haven, CT

    Over time, sludge and insoluble materials can accumulate in your brine tank, hindering the softening process. Our maintenance includes thoroughly cleaning the brine tank, inspecting for any cracks or leaks and confirming the float valve operates correctly. This step is essential for the efficient regeneration of your water softener in New Haven, CT.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in New Haven

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, controlling regeneration cycles and water flow. Our maintenance service includes complete diagnostics of the control valve's programming and mechanical components. We check for proper timing, motor function and potential blockages. If issues are found, we can often repair or replace worn parts to restore full function, preventing the need for a costly full system replacement in New Haven.

    Control Valve and Injector Service in New Haven, CT

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a full regeneration every time.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Maintenance in New Haven

    Template assisted crystallization and other salt-free conditioners do not remove hardness but change how it behaves. They still need media replacement every few years and sediment prefilter changes much more often. We inspect the media tank, replace prefilters and check bypass valves. We also explain honestly how these systems differ from a true softener.
