    Standard Hot Water Tank Tune-Up in New Haven

    GLV Plumbing offers a complete tune-up for conventional hot water tanks, focusing on critical components. This includes draining the tank to remove sediment buildup, which is crucial wherever water carries minerals, and inspecting the anode rod for signs of corrosion. We also check heating elements, thermostat settings and all pipe connections for leaks. Our goal is to improve your tank's performance and prevent future issues.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in New Haven, CT

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is vital for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Water chemistry varies from one neighborhood to the next, and it changes the rod's lifespan. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's badly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, saving you from a full tank replacement.

    Electric Hot Water Tank Element Check in New Haven

    Electric hot water tanks rely on heating elements to warm the water. During maintenance, our plumbers inspect these elements for signs of wear, corrosion or burnout. We also check the thermostat settings and wiring connections to ensure they are functioning correctly and safely. This preventative check can identify failing elements before they stop heating altogether, preventing unexpected cold showers for your family in New Haven.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in New Haven

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause noisy popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution rated for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Burner and Pilot Light Maintenance in New Haven, CT

    For gas hot water tanks, proper burner and pilot light operation is essential for efficient heating. Our service includes cleaning the burner assembly to remove dust and debris that can interfere with combustion. We also check and adjust the pilot light, making sure it ignites reliably. This attention to detail prevents common issues like intermittent hot water or complete heating failure, which can be frustrating for homeowners in New Haven, CT.

    Electric Element and Thermostat Service in New Haven

    Electric tanks rely on upper and lower heating elements that get crusted with scale in hard water. We test each element with a meter, check the thermostats and look for scorched wires at the terminal block. If an element has failed we replace it with the matching wattage and voltage. Many families who think they need a new heater just need a lower element and a flush.
