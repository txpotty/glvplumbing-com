    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Replacement in New Haven

    The cost for gas water heater replacement in New Haven, CT typically ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the unit size, brand, efficiency and any needed plumbing changes. Factors like gas line upgrades or ventilation improvements can impact the final price.

    Replacing a gas water heater involves working with gas lines, water connections and venting, which can be dangerous if not done correctly. It is strongly recommended to hire a licensed professional like GLV Plumbing for safety and code compliance.

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, banging or rumbling noises from the tank, leaks around the base or repeated pilot light outages. An aging unit, usually over 10 to 12 years old, is also a strong sign.

    Atmospheric vent water heaters use natural convection to vent combustion gases up a chimney or flue. Power vent models use an electric fan to force gases out, allowing for more flexible venting options and often higher efficiency due to more complete combustion.

    A typical gas water heater has a lifespan of 10 to 12 years with proper maintenance. Factors like water quality in New Haven, such as hard water causing mineral buildup, and usage patterns can affect its longevity.

    Most gas tanks last 8 to 12 years. Sediment from hard water and extreme garage temperatures can shorten that. Annual flushing and anode checks help them last longer.

    Regular maintenance, such as annual flushing to remove sediment and checking the anode rod, can greatly extend your gas water heater's lifespan. Proper sizing and professional installation also play a crucial role.

    Most new gas water heaters come with a manufacturer's warranty, usually covering the tank for 6 to 10 years and parts for 1 to 2 years. GLV Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation for extra peace of mind.
