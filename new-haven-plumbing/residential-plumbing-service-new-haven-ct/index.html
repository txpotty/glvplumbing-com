    We recommend a professional plumbing inspection every 1 to 2 years for most homes in New Haven, CT.     We recommend having your water heater inspected once a year, especially where hard water can speed up sediment buildup. Regular inspections can extend the lifespan of your unit, improve efficiency and help identify potential issues like anode rod corrosion or pressure relief valve problems before they become major repairs.

    Swapping a showerhead or toilet flapper is fine for most homeowners. Gas lines, water heaters and anything behind walls should be done by a licensed plumber for safety and code.

    Watch for persistent drips, slow drains, strange noises from pipes or water heaters, sudden drops in water pressure, discolored water or unexplained wet spots. Any of these signs indicate a potential problem that warrants a professional residential plumbing service check to prevent further damage.

    Warm spots on the floor, the sound of running water, a spinning meter with everything off and unexplained high water bills are common signs. Cracks in flooring or mildew smells can also point to a slab leak.

    While minor tasks like tightening a loose connection might be DIY-friendly, we generally advise against tackling complex plumbing repairs yourself.     To prevent issues, avoid pouring grease down drains, use drain strainers, don't flush anything but toilet paper and know where your main water shutoff valve is. Regular professional maintenance also helps catch problems early, which is especially important in homes with older plumbing systems.
