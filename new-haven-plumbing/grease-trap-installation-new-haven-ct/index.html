    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Installation in New Haven

    For high-volume commercial kitchens in New Haven, large outdoor grease interceptors are often required by code. These large units are installed underground and designed to manage significant amounts of FOG before wastewater enters the municipal sewer system. We handle the whole process, including excavation, precise placement, connection, and final inspection, delivering a robust and compliant installation.

    Grease Trap Installation Cost in New Haven, CT

    Small under-sink hydromechanical traps generally cost much less to install than large outdoor interceptors. Indoor units are typically a few thousand dollars installed, while in-ground interceptors can run well into five figures with excavation and concrete work. Pricing depends on unit size, location, soil conditions and whether we need to cut and patch floors. We give you a written estimate that includes permits and inspection. That way there are no surprises during construction.

    Gravity Grease Trap Installation in New Haven

    Gravity grease traps are a common and effective solution for many commercial kitchens in New Haven. These passive systems use the natural separation of FOG from water due to density differences. Our plumbers are experienced with gravity grease traps, ensuring proper sizing, correct inlet and outlet piping, and convenient access for future cleaning and maintenance to maximize their efficiency.

    Grease Trap Installation for Schools and Institutions in New Haven

    School cafeterias, hospitals, senior living facilities and churches with kitchens often need commercial interceptors. These sites usually have large dish machines and steam kettles that push high flow. We size interceptors to handle peak lunch or dinner loads without bypassing grease. Our crews schedule work around school breaks or off hours. We also provide documentation for facility managers and inspectors.

    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in New Haven

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with tight space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid easily.

    In-Ground Gravity Interceptor Installation in New Haven, CT

    Larger restaurants and high-volume kitchens often need an outdoor gravity interceptor. These concrete or polyethylene tanks typically range from several hundred to over 1,500 gallons. We excavate, set the tank on a stable base, connect inlet and outlet piping and install manhole risers. High water tables in low-lying areas of Connecticut can push groundwater into basements during wet seasons and overwork sump pumps. We plan excavation equipment around the ground conditions we find on site. A properly installed interceptor can go far longer between pump-outs than an indoor trap.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in New Haven, CT

    Hydro-mechanical grease traps, also known as automatic grease removal units, offer an active solution for FOG management in New Haven, CT. These units often include internal baffles and flow control mechanisms to improve separation efficiency. We provide professional installation of various hydro-mechanical models, ensuring all electrical and plumbing connections are correctly made for optimal performance and automated FOG removal.

    New Restaurant Build-Out Grease Traps in New Haven

    If you are opening a new restaurant, the grease trap is often a key part of plan review. We work with your architect or contractor to size the unit and show it on plumbing plans. We then install it during rough-in so it is ready for inspection. Getting the trap right early avoids costly changes after the slab is poured. We also coordinate with the city's pretreatment staff when they require a review.
