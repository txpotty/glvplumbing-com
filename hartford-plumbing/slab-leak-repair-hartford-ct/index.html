    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Hartford, CT

    When the leak is isolated and the rest of the pipe is in good shape, a spot repair through the slab is a practical option. We cut a small opening, excavate to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The trench is compacted and the concrete is patched. Flooring is then restored by your preferred contractor.

    Direct Slab Penetration Repair in Hartford, CT

    When a slab leak is directly accessible and localized, direct slab penetration is often the most practical repair method. This involves carefully cutting a small section of your concrete slab to access and repair or replace the damaged pipe section. Our team makes sure the repair is fully compliant with Connecticut plumbing codes and the slab is correctly restored, preventing future issues. We take great care to contain dust and debris during this process in your Hartford home.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Hartford

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is severely deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an effective long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely. This removes future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in Hartford with aging copper or galvanized pipes.

    Slab Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Hartford, CT

    Spot repairs usually have a lower upfront cost but involve breaking concrete and repairing flooring. Reroutes cost a bit more but avoid concrete work and future leaks in that line. Whole-house repipes are the best choice when you have had multiple slab leaks or old copper throughout. We give you clear prices for each option.
