    Yard and Outdoor Leak Detection in Hartford

    Leaks in outdoor plumbing, irrigation systems or main water lines buried in your yard can lead to wasted water and landscaping damage. We use ground-penetrating radar and tracer gas detection to locate buried pipes and identify leaks without extensive digging. Our approach minimizes disruption to your lawn and garden, preserving your property's appearance. Detecting these outdoor leaks early can also prevent erosion and potential foundation issues caused by saturated soil, a critical concern for homes in Hartford, CT.

    Pool Water Leak Detection in Hartford, CT

    Pools lose a lot of water to evaporation in summer heat, so a leak can hide behind normal loss. We use a bucket test to compare evaporation with actual water loss. Then we pressure test pool plumbing and dye test fittings, lights and skimmers. Finding the leak protects the pool and the soil around it.

    Slab Leak Detection in Hartford

    Slab leaks are common in homes on shifting soil and moving foundations. These leaks occur in pipes beneath your concrete slab, often signaled by warm spots on the floor, sudden spikes in your water bill or the sound of running water when all fixtures are off. GLV Plumbing uses dedicated acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to precisely locate these hidden leaks without destructive guesswork. Early detection is essential to preventing major foundation damage.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Hartford

    Leaks in underground water lines, from your meter to your home, can go unnoticed for extended periods, wasting water and causing yard erosion. Signs include unusually green patches in your lawn, persistent wet spots or a constantly running water meter. GLV Plumbing uses ground-penetrating radar and tracer gas detection methods to accurately map out and pinpoint leaks in buried pipes, allowing for targeted excavation and repair rather than digging up your entire yard.

    Toilet and Fixture Leak Detection in Hartford, CT

    Toilets are one of the most common sources of wasted water. A worn flapper or a fill valve that never shuts off can leak quietly around the clock. We use dye tests to find leaking toilets and check faucets, showers and supply lines for drips. These fixes are typically quick and save water right away.

    Hidden Wall & Ceiling Leak Detection in Hartford, CT

    Leaks behind walls or in ceilings can cause serious damage before they become visible, leading to stained drywall, peeling paint or a musty odor. These often stem from faulty pipe connections, old galvanized pipes or issues with shower pans and drains. Our technicians use moisture meters and infrared cameras to scan these areas, identifying elevated moisture levels and temperature differences that point to a hidden leak, minimizing the need for extensive wall demolition.
