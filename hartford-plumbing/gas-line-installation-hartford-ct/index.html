    Gas Water Heater and Tankless Line Upgrades in Hartford, CT

    Tankless water heaters often need up to 199,000 BTU, far more than the 40,000 BTU a typical tank heater uses. Many existing 1/2 inch lines cannot supply that demand, which causes error codes and lukewarm water. We calculate the full house load and upsize the line to 3/4 inch or larger as needed. This upgrade is one of the most critical steps in a successful tankless conversion.

    Flexible Gas Line (CSST) Installation in Hartford, CT

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing offers a flexible, efficient option for gas line installation. GLV Plumbing is proficient in installing CSST, which can simplify routing and reduce the number of fittings compared to traditional black iron pipe. We ensure CSST is properly bonded and grounded, as Connecticut code requires, to protect against lightning and electrical surges in your Hartford home.
