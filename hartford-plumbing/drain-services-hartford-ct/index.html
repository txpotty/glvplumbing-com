    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Services in Hartford

    Basic sink or tub drain cleaning typically costs $150 to $350. Main sewer line cleaning typically runs $250 to $600, and hydro jetting often costs $400 to $900 depending on line length and access.

    Routine drain cleaning and jetting do not require permits. Sewer line repair or replacement usually does, and we handle those permits.

    Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to clean the interior surfaces of your pipes, thoroughly removing grease, scale, and even tree roots. It is safe for most modern plumbing systems. Our technicians will perform a camera inspection first to ensure your pipes are structurally sound enough to withstand the process.

    While readily available, we usually advise against using chemical drain cleaners. They can be corrosive, potentially damaging your pipes, especially older ones, and are often ineffective against tough clogs like tree roots or heavy grease. They also pose a risk to your health and the environment. Professional methods are safer for your pipes and more effective.

    Hair, grease, soap scum and hard water scale cause most fixture clogs. Main line clogs are often caused by wipes, tree roots or a belly in the pipe.

    When it comes to drain services, our focus is on clearing and repairing the pipes themselves, rather than specific fixture brands. However, our technicians are familiar with all major plumbing fixture brands like Moen, Delta, Kohler, and American Standard, and can handle any issues related to their drain assemblies during service.

    For most homes, a professional drain cleaning every one to two years is enough for preventative maintenance. However, homes with older plumbing, frequent hair or grease buildup, or known tree root issues may benefit from annual cleaning. Businesses, especially restaurants, often require more frequent service.

    A typical drain cleaning for a single clogged fixture can take anywhere from 30 minutes to 2 hours, depending on the severity and location of the clog. More complex issues, like main sewer line clearing or hydro jetting, may require several hours. We provide an expected timeline after our initial assessment.
