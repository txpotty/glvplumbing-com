    New Sump Pit Installation in Hartford

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can create one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, hardpan or rock and set a durable basin. We add gravel around the basin to help water flow in. Then we patch the concrete and install the pump and cover. A new pit gives you a reliable place to collect and remove water.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Hartford, CT

    Power outages during heavy storms are a serious concern, which is why a battery backup sump pump is a smart addition. This system takes over on its own if your primary pump loses power, ensuring continuous flood protection. We install dependable backup units that can operate for hours, giving you peace of mind even when the electricity goes out in Hartford.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Hartford, CT

    For maximum peace of mind, consider a combination sump pump system. This setup includes a primary electric sump pump and an integrated battery backup pump in a single unit, providing full protection against both power failures and primary pump malfunctions. GLV Plumbing can install these dual-pump systems in your Hartford, CT home, offering superior flood prevention. This solution is a good fit for homes in flood-prone areas or those with valuable basement finishes.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Hartford

    A high water alarm alerts you when water rises above normal levels. Smart monitors can send alerts to your phone when the pump runs or fails. We install alarms and Wi-Fi monitors on new and existing pumps. These devices are especially useful for vacation homes and rental properties. Early warnings can prevent major water damage.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Hartford

    For complete protection, consider a combination sump pump system which integrates both a primary and a battery backup pump into one unit. These systems are pre-assembled for simpler installation and offer a compact solution for flood prevention. GLV Plumbing can install these efficient units, making sure your Hartford home has two layers of defense against water damage.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Hartford

    Some homes collect water in sunken patios, below-grade driveways or window wells rather than a basement.     A simple replacement often does not need a permit. New pits, electrical work or discharge connections may require one, and we will let you know.

    A submersible sump pump sits entirely inside the sump pit, submerged in water, making it quieter and taking up less space. A pedestal sump pump has its motor mounted on a shaft above the pit, making it easier to reach for maintenance and typically more affordable. Both types are effective, and our plumbers can help you choose the best fit for your Hartford basement.

    GLV Plumbing installs a variety of reputable sump pump brands known for their durability and performance. Our common selections include Zoeller, Little Giant, Wayne and Liberty Pumps. We can recommend the best brand and model to suit your particular needs and budget for your Hartford home.
