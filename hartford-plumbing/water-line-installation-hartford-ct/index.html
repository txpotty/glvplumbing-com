    PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Hartford, CT

    PEX and HDPE are flexible, which means fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement. HDPE is especially durable for long runs and resists cracking in expansive clay. We use approved compression or fusion fittings and add tracer wire so the line can be found with a locator later. PEX must stay out of sunlight before burial, so we store and lay it carefully on every job site.

    Commercial Water Service Installation in Hartford

    Restaurants, retail suites and small commercial buildings need service lines sized for higher demand and often require backflow protection at the meter. We install larger diameter copper or HDPE lines and coordinate with your water utility on tap and meter requirements. Our plumbers schedule work to limit downtime for tenants and customers. Inspection and backflow test reports are handled before the line goes into service.
