    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Hartford

    Undermount sinks need strong clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone, and support it while the adhesive sets. Then we install strainers, traps and supply lines. A proper undermount install helps prevent gaps and leaks along the counter edge.

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Hartford

    Top-mount, or drop-in, sinks are a common and straightforward option for many Connecticut homes. While simpler to install, proper sealing and alignment are still crucial to prevent water damage and ensure stability. Our plumbers in Hartford efficiently install top-mount sinks, applying high-quality sealant around the rim to create a watertight barrier. We make sure the sink is securely fastened and all plumbing connections, including the faucet and drain, are properly tightened and tested for leaks.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Hartford

    Double bowl sinks need a continuous waste or end outlet layout to drain both sides through one trap. We plan the layout around your disposal and dishwasher connections. Proper slope keeps both sides draining freely. We use heavy-wall fittings that resist leaks.
