    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Hartford

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to confirm a proper seal.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Hartford

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the distinct advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, typically using crimp or compression fittings. Our experts in Hartford can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Hartford, CT

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is notorious for sudden failures. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to sell and insure.
