    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Hartford, CT

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as necessary. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water fast. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.

    Attic and Garage Water Heater Maintenance in Hartford

    Many homes have water heaters in garages or attics, where heat, cold and tight access add stress to the unit. Attic units need working drain pans and drain lines to prevent ceiling damage if the tank leaks. We check the pan, drain line, venting and access platform. Garage units should be raised and protected where required for gas appliances. We make sure your setup meets local code.

    Water Heater Leak Detection and Prevention in Hartford, CT

    Leaks are a common issue that can lead to significant water damage if not caught early. Our maintenance service includes a thorough inspection of all water heater connections, pipes, and the tank itself for any signs of leaks or corrosion. We examine the drain pan and surrounding area for moisture. Early detection and repair of small leaks can prevent larger, more damaging problems, protecting your home's floors and interior from water damage.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Hartford

    Tankless units are especially sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. We isolate the unit with its service valves and circulate a descaling solution with a pump for the recommended time. We also clean the inlet water filter and check the unit's error history. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures steady.

    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Hartford

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Hartford, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's significantly depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing costly tank leaks.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Hartford

    For electric water heaters, our maintenance service focuses on checking heating elements, thermostats, and electrical connections. We confirm proper wiring and test for any short circuits or power problems that could affect performance. Sediment flushing is also performed, just like with gas models, to prevent buildup and protect the lower heating element from burning out prematurely in Hartford.
