    Sewage coming up through a tub, shower or floor drain is a health hazard and needs fast attention. Roots, grease and broken pipe are the most common causes.     Gas Line Emergencies in Norwich, CT

    A rotten egg odor, hissing near a gas appliance or a dead patch of grass over a buried line can point to a gas leak. Leave the home first and call the utility or 911 from a safe spot.     Emergency Sump Pump Service in Norwich

    Homes with basements or crawl spaces in flood-prone parts of Norwich rely on sump pumps. If your sump pump fails during heavy rain, it can lead to rapid flooding.
