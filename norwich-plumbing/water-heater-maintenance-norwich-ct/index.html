    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Norwich, CT

    Tankless water heaters need specialized maintenance, mainly a descaling flush to remove mineral deposits that can restrict flow and reduce efficiency. Our technicians in Norwich, CT use commercial-grade solutions to clean the heat exchanger, so your unit keeps providing endless hot water efficiently. We also inspect the igniter, fan, and exhaust venting for optimal performance and safety.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Norwich

    For electric water heaters, our maintenance service focuses on checking heating elements, thermostats, and electrical connections. We confirm proper wiring and test for any short circuits or power problems that could affect performance. Sediment flushing is also performed, just like with gas models, to prevent buildup and protect the lower heating element from burning out prematurely in Norwich.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Norwich

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part in your water heater designed to corrode instead of the tank itself. Depending on your water chemistry, these rods can wear down faster than you might think. During maintenance, we inspect the anode rod's condition and recommend replacement if necessary. A healthy anode rod is crucial for preventing rust and extending the lifespan of your water heater tank, saving you from a costly full replacement.

    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Norwich, CT

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building unsafe pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets swapped out. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge where installed.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Norwich, CT

    Gas water heater maintenance involves crucial safety inspections in Norwich, CT. We check for gas leaks, proper venting of exhaust gases, and the function of the pilot light or igniter. We also clean the burner assembly and inspect the thermocouple. These checks are essential to prevent carbon monoxide exposure and keep your gas-fired appliance running safely.

    Commercial Water Heater Maintenance in Norwich

    Businesses in Norwich rely on steady hot water, and our commercial water heater maintenance services are designed to meet those demands. We service larger capacity tanks and multiple unit setups with minimal disruption to your operations. Our technicians are experienced with commercial-grade systems and can schedule maintenance during off-peak hours to suit your business.

    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Norwich, CT

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as necessary. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water quickly. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.

    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Norwich

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely and efficiently. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for any leaks.
