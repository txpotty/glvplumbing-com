    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Norwich, CT

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is essential for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank.     Frequently Asked Questions About Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Norwich

    A standard flush and inspection typically runs $125 to $250 depending on tank size and access. Anode replacement typically adds $75 to $200 in parts and labor, and powered anodes cost more.

    Most manufacturers and plumbing experts recommend annual hot water tank maintenance. This is especially true where water is hard, since minerals speed up sediment buildup and anode rod depletion.

    The anode rod, also known as a sacrificial rod, is a metal rod inside your hot water tank designed to corrode before the tank itself. It protects the tank lining from rust and corrosion, which counts most when water carries a lot of minerals, and it lengthens the lifespan of your unit.

    Homeowners on well water near Norwich should test regularly, since acidic water can cause blue-green stains and pinhole leaks in copper.     Regular, professional maintenance can frequently be a condition for keeping your manufacturer's warranty valid. Check your warranty documentation, but generally, proof of annual service can help keep your warranty intact.

    A typical hot water tank maintenance service usually takes about 1 to 2 hours. This includes draining, flushing, inspection and any minor adjustments or part swaps like an anode rod.
