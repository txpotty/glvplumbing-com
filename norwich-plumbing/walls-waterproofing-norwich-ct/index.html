    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Norwich

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. High water tables in low-lying areas of Connecticut can push groundwater into basements during wet seasons and overwork sump pumps.     Crawl space encapsulation involves sealing the crawl space entirely with heavy-duty vapor barriers and often includes a dehumidifier.
