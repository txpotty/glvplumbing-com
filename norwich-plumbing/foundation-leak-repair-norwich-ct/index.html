    Foundation Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Norwich, CT

    Direct repairs are often the lowest upfront cost but involve breaking concrete and may need flooring repairs.     Water Line Reroute in Norwich

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This skips breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab.
