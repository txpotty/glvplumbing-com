    Cold water slab leaks are harder to feel but still waste water and wet the soil. They often show up as a high water bill or a meter that moves with all fixtures off. We isolate the cold line, find the leak and repair or reroute it. Fixing cold leaks quickly helps protect your foundation.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Norwich, CT

    In some cases, especially when the leak is under a load-bearing wall or in a difficult-to-access area, tunneling beneath the foundation is the preferred method.     Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Norwich, CT

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat expands and contracts the pipe against concrete and gravel. Mineral scale and constant recirculation can wear the pipe wall faster. We repair the leak or reroute the hot line, and we can adjust recirculation pumps to limit wear. Warm floors and higher gas or electric bills are common signs.
