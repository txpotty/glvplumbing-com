    Venting and Draft Repair in Norwich, CT

    Atmospheric gas water heaters depend on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and verify draft with a test. Proper venting is critical for your family's safety.

    Emergency Gas Water Heater Repair in Norwich

    When your gas water heater suddenly stops working, especially on a busy morning, it is an emergency. GLV Plumbing offers prompt emergency repair services for gas water heaters in Norwich. We put a priority on urgent calls to get your hot water back quickly, understanding that a lack of hot water is more than just an inconvenience.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Norwich, CT

    A yellow, lazy or noisy flame signals a dirty burner or poor combustion air. We remove and clean the burner, check the orifice size for natural gas or propane and adjust as needed. A clean blue flame heats faster and produces less carbon monoxide. We also check for rust flakes dropping from the flue.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Norwich, CT

    The gas control valve controls temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something goes wrong. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or listed universal model. We leak test and confirm temperature control afterward.

    Gas Water Heater Repair Cost in Norwich, CT

    Thermocouple and pilot repairs usually cost $150 to $350. Gas control valve replacement often runs $350 to $700, and power vent blower repairs can range from $400 to $800.     Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Norwich

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures usually involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent. We read the error codes, test each part and repair the fault. Correct vent slope and termination are verified before we leave.

    Pilot Light and Burner Issues in Norwich

    A frequent problem with gas water heaters is a pilot light that won't stay lit or a burner that fails to ignite. This can be caused by a faulty thermocouple, a dirty pilot tube or issues with the gas control valve. Our technicians in Norwich are adept at diagnosing these components and performing the necessary repairs, whether it is cleaning, adjusting or replacing parts. We ensure proper gas flow and ignition for consistent hot water.

    Pilot Light Repair in Norwich

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most frequent gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and service or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.
