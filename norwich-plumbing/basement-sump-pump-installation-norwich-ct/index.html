    Dependable Basement Sump Pump Installation Services in Norwich, CT

    New Sump Pit Excavation in Norwich

    If your basement has never had a pump, we start by locating the low point where water collects. We saw cut the slab, excavate a pit roughly 18 to 24 inches wide and 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a perforated or solid liner depending on whether we are collecting groundwater or surface seepage. Gravel goes around the liner to help water flow in while keeping fine soil out. We then patch the slab flush with new concrete so the floor remains clean and level.

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Norwich

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a solid fit for narrow pits in older homes. They typically last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we usually recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod set to your pit depth.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Norwich, CT

    Submersible sump pumps are designed to sit directly in the sump pit, submerged in water, which makes them quieter and less intrusive. They are powerful and effective for basements that take on a lot of water. Our team in Norwich, CT will install your submersible pump with precision, ensuring proper sealing, discharge piping and electrical connections for quiet, dependable operation, which matters most in finished basements.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Norwich

    Power outages during heavy storms can render a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump provides vital protection when the power goes out. We install reliable backup systems that activate automatically, keeping water moving even without electricity. This is an important safeguard for homes in Norwich that lose power during storms, offering peace of mind in severe weather. We tie these systems seamlessly with your existing plumbing.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Norwich

    A great pump is useless if the water it moves runs right back toward the foundation. We route rigid PVC discharge pipe out of the basement and extend it at least 10 feet from the house or into a pop-up emitter or dry well. A spring or swing check valve keeps the column of water from dropping back into the pit after every cycle, which saves wear on the motor. Outdoor faucets should be shut off from inside and drained each fall, and frost-free hose bibs are a worthwhile upgrade. Where lines can freeze, we add a freeze-resistant outlet so ice cannot block the discharge.

    Sump Pump Basin and Discharge Line Installation in Norwich

    A complete sump pump system needs a properly installed basin and discharge line. We handle the excavation and placement of the sump basin, setting it at the lowest point of your basement floor so it collects water effectively. We also install the discharge line, making sure it is correctly sloped, sealed and routed away from your foundation to a suitable drainage area, so water does not find its way back into your Norwich property.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Norwich, CT

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit is the least expensive option, typically a few hundred dollars plus the pump. Digging a new pit through the slab adds labor for saw cutting, excavation and concrete patching. Adding a battery backup raises the total but often costs less than one cleanup after a flooded basement. We give you an itemized quote so you can compare a basic primary pump against a full primary and backup system before we start.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Norwich

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit usually runs $500 to $1,200 including the pump and check valve. Installing a new pit through the slab generally falls between $1,500 and $3,500, and adding a battery backup adds about $600 to $1,500.

    Common signs you might need a new sump pump include unusual noises during operation, visible rust or corrosion on the unit, frequent cycling on and off, or a pump that fails to turn on during heavy rain. If your pump is more than 7 to 10 years old, it is also smart to consider a replacement before it fails completely.

    GLV Plumbing offers a comprehensive warranty on our basement sump pump installations, covering both parts and labor. The specific warranty period will be spelled out in your service agreement. We stand behind our work and the quality of the products we install in your Norwich home.

    We recommend having your sump pump inspected and maintained annually, ideally before the rainy season. Regular maintenance includes checking the float switch, cleaning debris out of the pit, testing the check valve and making sure the discharge line is clear. This helps prevent unexpected failures and extends the life of your pump.

    Replacing a pump usually does not need a permit, but new pits, new discharge routes or new electrical circuits may. We check local requirements and pull permits when they are needed.

    A standard basement sump pump installation typically takes 4 to 8 hours. If a new sump pit needs to be excavated, or there are unexpected challenges with plumbing or electrical access, the job might stretch to a full day. Our team works efficiently to minimize disruption to your home.
