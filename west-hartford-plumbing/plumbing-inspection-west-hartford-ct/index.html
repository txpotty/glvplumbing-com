    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in West Hartford, CT

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. High pressure above 80 psi shortens the life of fixtures and appliance hoses. We also identify supply pipe material, from copper and CPVC to polybutylene, Kitec and galvanized. Knowing what you have helps you plan repairs or a full repipe.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in West Hartford

    We check the water heater age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. We confirm proper venting, gas connections, expansion tanks and drain pans. Softeners and filtration systems get a basic function check and hardness test. Hard water reduces heater life, so these checks matter.

    Water Heater Inspections in West Hartford

    We carefully inspect all types of water heaters, including tankless, electric, and gas models. Our checks include anode rod condition, sediment buildup, pressure relief valves, and burner assembly for gas units. This helps your water heater operates efficiently and safely, preventing sudden breakdowns and extending its lifespan in West Hartford.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in West Hartford

    Public water in most of Connecticut runs soft to moderately hard, and soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper plumbing. Hard water can lead to mineral buildup in pipes, fixtures, and appliances. Our plumbing inspection includes assessing the extent of hard water damage, such as scale buildup in showerheads or water heaters, and can recommend appropriate water treatment solutions for your home in West Hartford.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in West Hartford, CT

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. We run a high-resolution camera from a cleanout to the city connection or septic tank. The video shows offsets, bellies, roots and breaks along with their depth and location. You keep a copy of the recording for your files.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspections in West Hartford, CT

    Using modern waterproof cameras, we conduct sewer line inspections to identify blockages, cracks, or tree root intrusion without invasive digging. This diagnostic tool is essential for detecting problems in underground lines that can lead to significant backups and damage, especially where shifting soil moves buried pipe.     Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in West Hartford, CT

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. We test angle stops and the main shutoff valve, which often seize over time. We also check for proper venting and gurgling drains. Small fixes like these are easy to plan for once you know they exist.
