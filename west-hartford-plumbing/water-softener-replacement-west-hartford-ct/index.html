    Metered Softener Replacement in West Hartford

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly used up. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first few weeks.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in West Hartford

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water all day and night. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand. They cost more upfront but prevent hard water bleed-through during regeneration.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in West Hartford

    A standard single tank softener replacement typically costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can raise the cost.

    Homeowners on well water near West Hartford should test regularly, since acidic water can cause blue-green stains and pinhole leaks in copper. Hard water contains dissolved minerals like calcium and magnesium. These minerals can lead to scale buildup in pipes, reduced efficiency of water heaters and appliances, spots on dishes, and dry skin and hair. A working water softener is essential for combating these issues in West Hartford homes.

    With proper maintenance, most quality water softeners can last between 10 and 15 years, and sometimes even longer. Factors like water hardness, usage volume and the quality of the original installation can affect its lifespan. Regular servicing helps extend the life of your unit.

    Spots on dishes, scale on faucets, dry skin and increased soap use can signal a failing softener. A softener that no longer uses salt or keeps flooding the brine tank may also need replacement.

    Requirements vary by city. Simple replacements may not require one, but new plumbing connections may. We check local rules on your behalf.

    We test your hardness in grains per gallon and estimate daily water usage. That tells us the resin capacity needed.
