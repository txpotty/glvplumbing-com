    Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in West Hartford, CT

    Many modern plumbing systems include an expansion tank connected to the water heater. This tank takes up excess pressure created during the heating cycle, protecting your hot water tank and other plumbing fixtures. Our maintenance includes checking the expansion tank's air pressure and overall condition. Proper expansion tank function is essential to preventing premature wear on your hot water tank and maintaining system integrity.

    Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in West Hartford, CT

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial protector against corrosion. Public water in most of Connecticut runs soft to moderately hard, and soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper plumbing. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod. If it's badly depleted, we recommend and perform a replacement to extend the life of your tank in West Hartford, CT, preventing rust from eating away at the tank lining.

    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in West Hartford

    A properly working temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to ensure it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will suggest immediate replacement to keep your West Hartford home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.

    Standard Hot Water Tank Flushing in West Hartford

    Sediment buildup is a common issue in hot water tanks, especially where water hardness runs high. Our standard flushing service removes these mineral deposits, which can reduce efficiency and lead to premature tank failure. We drain the tank completely, flush out all sediment and refill it, ensuring clear water flow and improved heating performance. This routine service is crucial for extending the life of your unit.

    Sediment Flushing & Cleaning in West Hartford, CT

    Over time, minerals and sediment settle at the bottom of hot water tanks, reducing efficiency and possibly leading to premature failure.     Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in West Hartford, CT

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is vital for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Water chemistry varies from one neighborhood to the next, and it changes the rod's lifespan. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's badly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, sparing you a full tank replacement.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in West Hartford, CT

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can clog the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a stable blue flame and lower gas bills.
