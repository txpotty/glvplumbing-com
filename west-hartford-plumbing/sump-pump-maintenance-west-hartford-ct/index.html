    Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in West Hartford

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last longer.

    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in West Hartford, CT

    The float switch is a key component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to ensure reliable operation for your home in West Hartford, CT.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in West Hartford, CT

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Many homes with crawlspaces or deep window wells use small pumps to clear runoff. We service these smaller units, clear their screens and confirm the discharge reaches daylight or an approved drain. When a deep cold snap is forecast, keeping the heat steady and opening cabinets on outside walls can help prevent a burst pipe.

    Battery Backup System Testing in West Hartford, CT

    A power outage during a heavy storm can render your primary sump pump useless. Our maintenance service includes complete testing of your battery backup sump pump system.     Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in West Hartford
