    Jet Pump System Repair in West Hartford, CT

    Jet pumps, typically found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Typical problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. GLV Plumbing can reliably troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in West Hartford operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is essential for jet pump function.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in West Hartford, CT

    Variable speed systems like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by adjusting motor speed. When they fault out, the drive usually shows a code that guides diagnosis. We troubleshoot drives, sensors and wiring and repair or replace components. These systems need a smaller pressure tank but careful setup.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in West Hartford

    Submersible pumps are common in residential wells, designed to operate submerged within the well casing. When these pumps fail, symptoms often include no water, low water pressure, or strange noises from the well. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like motor burnout, damaged impellers, or faulty check valves that can affect submersible pump performance. We use specialized equipment for safe and efficient pump extraction and reinstallation, ensuring the integrity of your well system.

    Well Pump Control Box & Electrical Repair in West Hartford, CT

    Many well pump failures trace back to electrical issues within the control box or wiring. This can include tripped breakers, faulty capacitors, or corroded connections. Our licensed technicians are trained to safely diagnose and repair these complex electrical components. We ensure all wiring meets Connecticut electrical codes, preventing hazards and ensuring reliable pump operation in West Hartford homes.
