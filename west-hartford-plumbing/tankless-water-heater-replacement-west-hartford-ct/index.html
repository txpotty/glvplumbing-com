    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in West Hartford, CT

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. These units face sun, rain and temperature swings, which can wear out plastic covers and wiring over time. Frozen pipes are a regular winter risk in Connecticut, especially lines in exterior walls, crawlspaces, and unheated porches or garages. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to shield the water and gas lines. Exterior models are often the simplest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in West Hartford
