    Reliable Backflow Preventer Installation Services in Meriden, CT

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Meriden, CT

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested. We carry trusted brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Commercial Backflow Preventer Installation in Meriden

    Restaurants, salons, dental offices and light industrial buildings often need multiple backflow devices on soda machines, boilers, dishwashers and process water. We survey the building, identify each cross-connection and install containment and isolation assemblies as required. Work is scheduled after hours or on weekends when needed to avoid shutting down your business. You receive documentation for each device for your records and your water utility.

    Backflow Prevention for Irrigation Systems in Meriden, CT

    Irrigation systems in Meriden, CT are a common source of backflow, as stagnant water and lawn chemicals can be drawn back into the potable water supply. Installing a dedicated backflow preventer for your sprinkler system is often a local code requirement. We are experienced in selecting and installing the correct device, such as a pressure vacuum breaker or atmospheric vacuum breaker, to protect your home's main water line from contaminants coming from your landscape irrigation.

    Commercial Backflow Prevention in Meriden, CT

    Commercial properties in Meriden, CT often have complex plumbing systems that pose a higher risk of cross-contamination and need robust backflow prevention. Restaurants, hospitals, industrial facilities and even multi-family buildings must comply with commercial plumbing codes. GLV Plumbing provides full installation of heavy-duty backflow preventers, including large diameter RPZ assemblies, to safeguard public health and meet Connecticut regulations for businesses.     Pool and Spa Fill Backflow Protection in Meriden, CT

    An auto-fill line or hose connection on a pool or spa creates a straight path for chlorinated water to reach the supply. We install the correct device on pool fill lines and check that hose bibbs have vacuum breakers. This simple step protects your household water and keeps you in line with local code. We can add the work when a pool is being built or renovated.

    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in Meriden, CT

    Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights. Outdoor faucets should be shut off from inside and drained each fall, and frost-free hose bibs are a smart upgrade. We install insulated enclosures, freeze blankets or heated cabinets based on your location. Enclosures also discourage theft, since brass backflow assemblies are a common target for scrap thieves. We choose enclosures with enough room for testing and repairs.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Meriden

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules permit. We size them to avoid too much pressure loss on your system.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Meriden

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a widely used backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Meriden, DCVAs are frequently installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers carefully install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer Installation in Meriden

    The cost for backflow preventer installation in Meriden, CT typically ranges from $400 to $1,500, depending on the type of device, its size and the complexity of the installation. A basic residential PVB for an irrigation system is usually on the lower end, while a commercial-grade RPZ assembly costs more because of material and labor requirements.

    Backflow preventers are installed at cross-connection points where non-potable water could enter the potable water supply. Common locations include irrigation system connections, fire sprinkler lines, boiler systems and connections to auxiliary water supplies, often just after the main water meter or at the point of hazard.

    Most residential installs take two to four hours including testing. Large commercial assemblies can take a full day, especially if piping must be reconfigured.

    Most municipalities require annual testing of backflow preventers by a licensed, certified backflow tester. This confirms the device is working correctly and continues to protect your potable water supply from contamination. We can also assist with scheduling these annual tests.

    Most manufacturers offer one to five year warranties depending on the model. We also warranty our installation labor and will go over the coverage with you.

    Constant dripping or discharge from an RPZ relief valve, low pressure downstream and a failed annual test are typical signs. Visible corrosion or a cracked body from freezing also means service is needed.

    In Meriden, most backflow preventers must be tested annually by a certified backflow tester to confirm they are working correctly and providing adequate protection. This is a separate service from installation but is crucial for ongoing compliance and safety.
