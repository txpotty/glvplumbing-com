    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Meriden

    The cost of well pump replacement in Meriden, CT usually ranges from $1,500 to $3,500, though it can vary. Factors influencing the price include the type of pump, well depth, horsepower and any additional components needed like pressure tanks or wiring upgrades. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after a thorough assessment.

    Shallow well pumps, or jet pumps, are designed for wells where the water level is typically 25 feet or less from the pump, using suction to draw water. Deep well pumps, or submersible pumps, are placed directly in the water near the bottom of the well and push water up, suited for depths over 25 feet. Choosing the correct type is critical for efficiency and performance.

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up from shallower wells, usually less than 25 feet. Submersible pumps are placed directly inside the well casing, submerged in water, and are used for deeper wells, pushing water up to the surface. Each has its own applications and benefits.

    We install Grundfos, Goulds and Franklin Electric pumps. These brands are dependable and widely supported.

    If your pressure tank is old or waterlogged, replacing it at the same time can protect the new pump. A failed tank causes rapid short cycling, which is one of the most frequent reasons submersible motors burn out early.

    In many areas, a plumbing or well permit is required for well pump replacement to confirm compliance with local building and health codes. GLV Plumbing handles all required permitting on your behalf, so your installation is fully compliant and safe.

    Low pressure, sputtering faucets, no water and high electric bills can point to pump trouble. Frequent cycling is another warning sign.

    Most standard well pump replacements can be completed within a single day, typically 4 to 8 hours. However, complex installations involving very deep wells, difficult access or additional system repairs may take longer. Our team works quickly to restore your water as soon as possible.
