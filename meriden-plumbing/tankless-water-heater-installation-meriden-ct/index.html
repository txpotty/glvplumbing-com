    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Meriden, CT

    Calcium scale is the most common cause of tankless heater failure. A large share of homes in Connecticut use private wells, which can be acidic or carry iron, manganese, or radon that require treatment. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides added protection.
