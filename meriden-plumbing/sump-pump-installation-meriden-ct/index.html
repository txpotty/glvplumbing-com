    New Sump Pit Installation in Meriden

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can build one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, compacted clay or rock and set a durable basin.     Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Meriden

    Submersible sump pumps sit directly inside the sump pit, operating quietly and taking up less space.
