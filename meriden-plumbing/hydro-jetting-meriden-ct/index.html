    Hydro jetting is highly effective against a wide range of stubborn clogs that traditional snaking cannot fully clear.     While hydro jetting is very powerful, it is usually safe for most sound plumbing systems, including PVC, cast iron, and concrete pipes.     A thorough jetting often keeps a line clear for a year or more, depending on use and roots. Lines with heavy root intrusion may require maintenance or a repair.
