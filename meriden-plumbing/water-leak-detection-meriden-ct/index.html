    Slab Leak Detection in Meriden

    Slab leaks are common in homes on shifting soil and moving foundations. These leaks occur in pipes beneath your concrete slab, often indicated by warm spots on the floor, sudden spikes in your water bill or the sound of running water when all fixtures are off. GLV Plumbing uses specialized acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to precisely locate these hidden leaks without destructive guesswork. Early detection is key to preventing major foundation damage.

    Leak Detection for PEX and Copper Pipes in Meriden

    Whether your plumbing system uses modern PEX tubing or traditional copper pipes, GLV Plumbing has the expertise to detect leaks in both materials. PEX, while flexible, can be susceptible to damage from rodents or improper installation, while copper can corrode over time, especially with hard water. Our tools are effective across various pipe materials, ensuring accurate detection regardless of your home's plumbing type.

    Hidden Wall & Ceiling Leak Detection in Meriden, CT

    Leaks behind walls or in ceilings can cause significant damage before they become visible, leading to stained drywall, peeling paint or a musty odor. These often stem from faulty pipe connections, old galvanized pipes or issues with shower pans and drains. Our technicians use moisture meters and infrared cameras to scan these areas, identifying elevated moisture levels and temperature differences that point to a hidden leak, minimizing the need for extensive wall demolition.

    Pool Water Leak Detection in Meriden, CT

    Pools lose a lot of water to evaporation in summer heat, so a leak can hide behind normal loss. We use a bucket test to compare evaporation with actual water loss. Then we pressure test pool plumbing and dye test fittings, lights and skimmers. Finding the leak safeguards the pool and the soil around it.
