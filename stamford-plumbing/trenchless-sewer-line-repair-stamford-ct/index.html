    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Stamford

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while fracturing the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is very resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Stamford homes. This method is ideal when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Sewer Camera Inspections for Trenchless Repair in Stamford

    Before any trenchless repair, a detailed sewer camera inspection is essential. Our high-definition cameras travel through your sewer lines, providing real-time video of the pipe's interior. This diagnostic step makes for a precise and effective repair in Stamford.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Stamford, CT

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is particularly useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add decades of service.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Stamford

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We identify the material on camera before recommending a method.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Cost in Stamford, CT

    Point repairs often cost $1,500 to $3,500, while full lining of a residential lateral commonly runs $4,000 to $12,000 depending on length and diameter. Pipe bursting typically ranges from $5,000 to $15,000. Traditional excavation can be cheaper upfront on short, shallow runs, but restoring concrete and landscaping can quickly close the gap.
