    Complete Tankless Water Heater Maintenance Services in Stamford, CT

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Stamford

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system completely. Restored flow means more stable temperatures and fewer shutdowns.

    Isolation Valve Kit Installation in Stamford, CT

    Some tankless heaters were installed without service valves, which makes proper flushing impossible. We install a set of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines so future maintenance takes minutes. The kit also lets you shut off water to the heater without closing the main. It is a one-time upgrade that pays for itself in simpler service.
