    Pinhole leaks are tiny holes that form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher. We cut out the affected section and replace it with new Type L copper or a PEX transition. While the wall is open, we inspect nearby pipe for discoloration or thinning.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Copper Pipe Leak Repair in New Britain

    The cost of copper pipe leak repair in New Britain can vary a lot depending on the leak's location, severity and accessibility. Minor repairs, like a simple solder patch, might range from $200 to $500. More complex issues, such as a slab leak requiring specialized detection and repair, could range from $800 to $3,000 or more. We provide a detailed estimate after our initial inspection.

    Common causes include aggressive water chemistry, high water velocity, excessive pressure, poor original soldering and stray electrical current from grounding issues. These factors erode the pipe wall from the inside over time.

    Most simple copper pipe leak repairs, such as fixing an accessible pinhole leak or a single joint, can be completed within one to four hours. More complex issues, like leaks under a slab or extensive re-piping, may take a full day or sometimes multiple days, depending on the scope of work.

    To prevent future copper pipe leaks, consider installing a whole-house water softener if you have hard water, as mineral buildup can lead to corrosion. Maintaining appropriate water pressure, insulating pipes for cold weather and scheduling periodic plumbing inspections can also greatly extend the life of your copper pipes.

    While temporary fixes like clamps or epoxy putties exist, professional repair is the better choice for lasting results. DIY repairs often fail, leading to more significant water damage and costly secondary issues. Licensed plumbers have the expertise, tools and materials for a durable, code-compliant repair that DIY efforts cannot match.

    Insurance often covers sudden water damage but not the cost of repairing the pipe itself. Review your policy, and we will provide documentation to help with a claim.

    PEX resists the corrosion and scale that cause copper pinholes and is flexible and quick to install. If you have repeated copper leaks, repiping with PEX is usually a cost-effective long-term solution.
