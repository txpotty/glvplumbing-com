    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in New Britain

    Replacing a pump in an existing basin usually costs $900 to $2,000. A new ejector system with basin, venting and floor work typically ranges from $2,500 to $6,000 or more.

    Pump replacements usually take two to four hours. New systems with floor excavation and venting typically take one to two days.

    New ejector installations typically require a plumbing permit and inspection. Straight pump replacements may not, and we confirm local requirements for every job.

    We install Zoeller, Liberty Pumps and Wayne ejector pumps, plus Saniflo macerating systems. We choose the brand and model based on your fixtures and lift height.

    We recommend having your sewage ejector pump inspected annually, especially if it is in a high-use area. Regular inspections can identify potential issues early, extend the life of your pump and prevent unexpected failures and costly backups. This is especially important for older units.

    A sump pump is designed to remove groundwater from a basement or crawl space, preventing flooding. A sewage ejector pump, however, is built specifically to handle wastewater containing solids from toilets, sinks and showers in areas below the main sewer line. They are not interchangeable and serve separate purposes.

    Manufacturer warranties typically range from 1 to 5 years depending on the model. We also warranty our installation labor.

    Regular maintenance for a sewage ejector pump involves occasionally checking the basin for debris, confirming the check valve works correctly and testing the float switch. It is also wise to avoid flushing non-biodegradable items. GLV Plumbing offers maintenance services to keep your pump running efficiently and prevent surprise breakdowns.
