    Badger Series Disposal Installation in New Britain

    The InSinkErator Badger line is the most common replacement disposal in area kitchens. These 1/3 to 3/4 HP units handle everyday scraps and fit most standard cabinets.     Septic-Friendly Disposal Installs in New Britain, CT

    Homes on septic systems need a disposal that does not overload the tank. Septic systems are common in Connecticut, and many towns outside the larger urban areas have little or no public sewer service. InSinkErator's Septic Assist model injects an enzyme solution to help break down food waste. We install the unit, mount the enzyme cartridge and explain how often to replace it.     Evolution Series Upgrades in New Britain, CT

    Evolution models like the Compact, Septic Assist and Excel use multi-stage grinding and extra sound insulation. They break down fibrous foods such as celery and corn husks that can jam lighter units. We install Evolution disposals for families who cook often and want a quieter kitchen. Because these units are taller and heavier, we check cabinet clearance and trap height before mounting. The result is a smoother grind and fewer clogs downstream.

    Disposal Replacement and Haul-Away in New Britain, CT

    When an old disposal hums, leaks from the bottom or trips the reset button constantly, replacement is usually smarter than repair. We haul the old unit away so you are not stuck disposing of it. Most replacements finish in a single visit.
