    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in New Britain

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for extensive trenching. It is ideal for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in New Britain that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Cost Comparison of Trenchless vs Traditional in New Britain, CT

    Pipe Bursting in New Britain, CT

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is best for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It usually requires an entry pit and an exit pit.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in New Britain, CT

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is excellent for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a quick and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Connecticut homeowners.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in New Britain, CT

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is especially useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add decades of service.
