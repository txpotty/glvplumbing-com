    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in New Britain, CT

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, similar to a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when several fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to weigh cost and performance.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in New Britain, CT

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a secure seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can be repaired from kinks with a heat gun. It is our favored choice for many full repipes.

    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in New Britain

    If your home has old, corroded or leaking pipes made of galvanized steel or polybutylene, a whole-home PEX repipe is an excellent solution.     PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in New Britain

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a solid option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer dependable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to confirm a proper seal.

    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in New Britain

    Kitchen and bathroom remodels are a good time to replace old supply lines with PEX. We rough in new lines to the correct heights for vanities, showers, tubs and appliances, then pressure test before the walls are closed. Additions and guest suites get correctly sized lines tied into your existing system. We coordinate with your contractor to keep the project schedule moving.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in New Britain

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, typically using crimp or compression fittings. Our plumbers in New Britain can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.
