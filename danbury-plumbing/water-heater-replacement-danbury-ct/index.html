    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Danbury

    The cost of water heater replacement in Danbury, CT usually ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of unit (gas, electric, tankless or hybrid), its capacity and any needed plumbing modifications. Our team will provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your needs and existing setup.

    Most standard tank-style water heater replacements can be completed in 3 to 5 hours, assuming no major complications or plumbing reconfigurations. Tankless or hybrid installations might take a bit longer, often 5 to 8 hours, due to more complex venting or gas line changes.

    A traditional water heater stores and heats a limited amount of water in a tank, whereas a tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit. Tankless units offer endless hot water and can be more energy-efficient, but usually have a higher upfront cost and may require gas line upgrades.

    Choosing the right size depends on the number of people in your household, their hot water usage habits and peak demand times. Our technicians will calculate the right GPM (gallons per minute) for tankless or gallon capacity for tank models so you never run out of hot water, especially in multi-bathroom Danbury homes.

    Many households of two to three people do well with a 40 gallon tank, while larger families often need 50 gallons or more. We size based on your bathroom count and usage.

    Most replacements take two to four hours. Attic installs or relocations can take longer.

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, a metallic taste in hot water, strange noises like rumbling or popping from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit or a water heater that is over 10 to 12 years old. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a licensed plumber.

    GLV Plumbing installs and services all major brands, including Bradford White, Rheem, AO Smith, Rinnai, Navien and State Water Heaters. We can help you choose the best brand and model that fits your budget and hot water demands.
