    Comfort-Height Toilet Replacement in Danbury

    Replacing a standard-height toilet with a comfort-height model raises the seat by about 2 to 3 inches. That small change makes a real difference for seniors and anyone with knee or back issues. We often pair this upgrade with an elongated bowl and soft-close seat. The swap uses the same drain location, so no extra plumbing work is required.

    Water-Saving Toilet Upgrades in Danbury, CT

    Older toilets are one of the biggest indoor water users in a home. WaterSense toilets use 1.28 gallons per flush or less, and dual-flush models add a lighter flush for liquids. We recommend models with high MaP scores so you save water without double flushing. Ask your water provider whether it now offers a toilet rebate.

    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Danbury, CT

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, which means fewer leak points and simpler cleaning. They cost more and weigh more, but many homeowners like the clean look. We install one-piece models from TOTO, Kohler and American Standard. Our team handles the extra weight with care to protect your floors.
