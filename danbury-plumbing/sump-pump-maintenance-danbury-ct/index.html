    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Danbury

    Heavy downpours can overload older sewer systems and push water back into basements, so a backwater valve can be a sensible upgrade. We test your pump ahead of the rainy season by filling the pit until the float trips. We watch the pump empty the basin, listen for grinding bearings and measure how fast it cycles. If the pump struggles, you still have time to fix it before the first big storm.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in Danbury, CT

    For homes equipped with pedestal sump pumps, GLV Plumbing provides specialized maintenance services in Danbury, CT. We focus on inspecting the external motor, confirming proper lubrication and checking the impeller for any obstructions. This type of pump, with its motor above the water, requires specific attention to its shaft and bearings to prevent premature wear and support reliable performance during heavy rains.

    Battery Backup Pump Maintenance in Danbury

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails. We load test the battery, check the charger and alarm and run the backup pump on its own. Most backup batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Danbury

    GLV Plumbing is experienced in servicing Zoeller sump pumps, a widely trusted brand known for its durability.     Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Danbury, CT

    Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Danbury

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last longer.

    Commercial and Elevator Pit Pumps in Danbury

    Commercial buildings often have sump pumps in elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms. These pumps may need oil-minder controls or alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. We test duplex systems, alternating controls and high-water alarms on a scheduled basis. Maintenance logs help property managers stay on top of inspections.
