    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Danbury

    Replacing a pump in an existing basin usually costs $900 to $2,000. A new ejector system with basin, venting and floor work typically ranges from $2,500 to $6,000 or more.

    We mainly install high-quality, reputable brands such as Liberty Pumps and Zoeller. These manufacturers are known for their durability, reliability and efficient performance, providing a long-lasting solution for your home. We can discuss the best brand and model for your needs.

    Common signs that you might need a new sewage ejector pump include the pump running constantly, not turning on at all, making unusual grinding or loud noises, or foul odors or sewage backing up into your basement fixtures. These symptoms point to a failing unit that needs immediate attention.

    We recommend having your sewage ejector pump inspected annually, especially if it is in a high-use area. Regular inspections can identify potential issues early, extend the life of your pump and prevent unexpected failures and costly backups. This is particularly important for older units.

    With proper installation and regular maintenance, a high-quality sewage ejector pump can last 7 to 15 years. Factors such as usage frequency, the type of waste it handles and water quality can affect its lifespan. Annual inspections can help extend its longevity.

    New ejector installations typically require a plumbing permit and inspection. Straight pump replacements may not, and we confirm local requirements for every job.

    A sump pump is designed to remove groundwater from a basement or crawl space, preventing flooding. A sewage ejector pump, however, is built specifically to handle wastewater containing solids from toilets, sinks and showers in areas below the main sewer line.     Regular maintenance for a sewage ejector pump involves periodically checking the basin for debris, confirming the check valve works correctly and testing the float switch.
