    Gas and Electric Water Heater Checks in Danbury, CT

    Whether your home in Danbury, CT uses a gas or electric hot water tank, our maintenance covers the specific needs of each. For gas units, we inspect burner assemblies, gas lines and vent systems for safety and efficiency. For electric units, we check heating elements, wiring and thermostats. Our detailed checks ensure all energy sources are operating correctly and safely.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Danbury

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure spike every heating cycle. High street pressure is not unusual in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Danbury, CT

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can block the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a steady blue flame and lower gas bills.

    Standard Hot Water Tank Flushing in Danbury

    Sediment buildup is a common issue in hot water tanks, especially where water hardness runs high. Our standard flushing service removes these mineral deposits, which can reduce efficiency and lead to premature tank failure. We drain the tank completely, flush out all sediment and refill it, giving you clear water flow and improved heating performance. This routine service is key for extending the life of your unit.

    Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Danbury, CT

    Many homes put the water heater in a garage or attic where temperatures can swing hard. Heat speeds up the wear on plastic drain valves, gas valve seals and wiring insulation. We check drain pans, pan drain lines, platform height for gas units in garages and clearances around the tank. A leak in an attic tank without a working pan can ruin a ceiling, so we confirm that protection is in place.

    Emergency Hot Water Tank Inspections in Danbury

    If you notice sudden changes in your hot water tank's performance, like strange noises, discolored water or a sudden drop in hot water supply, GLV Plumbing offers emergency inspections in Danbury. While not a full maintenance, we can quickly diagnose the issue and advise on immediate steps. That usually includes a focused maintenance procedure to restore function and prevent further damage.

    Standard Hot Water Tank Tune-Up in Danbury

    GLV Plumbing offers a complete tune-up for conventional hot water tanks, focusing on critical components. This includes draining the tank to remove sediment buildup, which is essential wherever water carries minerals, and inspecting the anode rod for signs of corrosion. We also check heating elements, thermostat settings and all pipe connections for leaks. Our goal is to improve your tank's performance and prevent future issues.

    Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in Danbury, CT

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial defense against corrosion. A large share of homes in Connecticut use private wells, which can be acidic or carry iron, manganese, or radon that require treatment. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod. If it's badly depleted, we recommend and perform a replacement to extend the life of your tank in Danbury, CT, preventing rust from eating away at the tank lining.
