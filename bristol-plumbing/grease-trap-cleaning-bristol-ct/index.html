    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Cleaning in Bristol

    Small indoor grease traps typically cost $150 to $400 per cleaning. Large outdoor interceptors often run $300 to $1,200 depending on size and buildup. Regular maintenance plans can lower the cost per visit.

    FOG stands for Fats, Oils, and Grease. When poured down drains, FOG cools and solidifies, sticking to pipe walls and eventually leading to severe clogs. In grease traps, it separates from wastewater, but if not regularly removed, it overflows into the sewer system, causing blockages and environmental issues.

    GLV Plumbing services all common types of grease traps and interceptors, including passive hydromechanical grease interceptors (HGIs), automatic grease removal units (AGRUs), and large gravity grease interceptors (GGIs). We handle both indoor under-sink units and outdoor ground-level tanks.

    Generally, you do not need a permit to have your existing grease trap cleaned by a professional. However, commercial establishments in Bristol are often required to maintain records of their grease trap cleaning for compliance audits. New grease trap installations may require plumbing permits.

    The cleaning frequency depends heavily on your business's FOG output and the size of your trap. Most restaurants require cleaning every 1 to 3 months, while high-volume establishments might need it monthly. Local health codes in Bristol might also specify minimum frequencies.
