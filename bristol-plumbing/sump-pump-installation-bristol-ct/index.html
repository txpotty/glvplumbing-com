    A submersible sump pump sits inside the sump pit, making it quieter and more powerful, well suited for heavy use. A pedestal sump pump has its motor above the pit, making it easier to reach for maintenance and often more affordable, though it can be louder.

    A submersible sump pump sits entirely inside the sump pit, submerged in water, making it quieter and taking up less space. A pedestal sump pump has its motor mounted on a shaft above the pit, making it easier to reach for maintenance and typically more affordable.
