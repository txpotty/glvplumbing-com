    Frequently Asked Questions About Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Bristol

    Replacing a spout or trim kit usually costs $150 to $350. A cartridge or stem swap runs $175 to $400, and a full in-wall valve replacement typically ranges from $450 to $1,200 depending on access and tile.

    We install and replace all major brands, including Moen, Delta, Kohler, American Standard, Pfister, and more. Our plumbers are familiar with the specific installation requirements for a broad range of manufacturers.

    Spout and trim swaps take about an hour. Cartridge replacement takes one to two hours, and a full valve replacement may take between three and six hours.

    Faucet repair involves fixing a specific component, like a washer or cartridge, to stop a leak or improve function. Replacement means installing an entirely new faucet fixture. We recommend replacement when the faucet is old, extensively corroded, or has multiple issues that make repair uneconomical.

    There are several types, including single-handle, double-handle, Roman tub fillers, and wall-mounted faucets. Each offers different style and functional benefits. We can help you choose the best type for your particular bathtub and bathroom design.
