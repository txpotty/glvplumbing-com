    Understanding Polybutylene Failure in Bristol

    Polybutylene's inherent weakness stems from its chemical reaction with oxidants like chlorine, commonly used in municipal water treatment. Over time, this reaction causes the pipes and their plastic fittings to become brittle, leading to hairline cracks and eventual rupture. Public water in most of Connecticut runs soft to moderately hard, and soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper plumbing. Hard water can also add stress to pipe that is already weakened. Understanding this failure mechanism is crucial for diagnosing issues and recommending the most effective and permanent replacement strategy for your home.
