    Common signs you might need new water lines in your Bristol home include consistently low water pressure, discolored water (especially reddish-brown), frequent leaks, or very old pipes (like galvanized steel).     In Bristol, most backflow preventers are required to be tested annually by a certified technician to ensure they are functioning correctly. This is essential for maintaining the safety of your public water supply. GLV Plumbing offers convenient annual testing and certification services.

    A sump pump is designed to remove clear groundwater that collects in a sump pit, usually in a basement or crawl space, to prevent flooding. A sewage ejector pump, on the other hand, handles wastewater containing solids from plumbing fixtures located below the main sewer line, lifting it up to the main sewer or septic system.
