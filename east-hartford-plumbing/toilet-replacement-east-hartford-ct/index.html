    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Replacement in East Hartford

    The cost of toilet replacement in East Hartford, CT typically ranges from $400 to $800, including the new toilet fixture and labor. This can vary based on the toilet model you choose, any necessary repairs to the flange or subfloor, and the difficulty of the installation.

    While it is possible to DIY a toilet replacement, we highly recommend hiring a professional. Improper installation can lead to leaks, water damage, and costly repairs. Our plumbers make sure of correct sealing, proper flange connection, and adherence to all plumbing codes.

    Key signs include persistent leaks that are difficult to pinpoint or fix, a cracked toilet bowl or tank, frequent clogs despite proper use, excessive rocking or instability, or a toilet that is very old and inefficient, using too much water per flush. If repair costs approach the price of a new toilet, replacement is usually the better choice.

    We frequently recommend and install reliable brands like American Standard, Kohler, Toto, and Delta. These brands offer a range of durable, water-efficient models that perform well across a wide range of water conditions and are known for their long service life.

    Most replacements take 1.5 to 2.5 hours. If the flange or subfloor needs repair, it may take longer.

    Signs of failing internal toilet parts include a toilet that runs constantly, a weak or incomplete flush, a handle that needs jiggling, or visible wear and tear on components like the flapper, fill valve, or flush valve. While some parts can be replaced one at a time, an older toilet with multiple failing components might benefit more from a full replacement.

    Public water in most of Connecticut runs soft to moderately hard, and soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper plumbing. Hard water can lead to mineral buildup in toilet tanks and bowls, affecting flush mechanisms and causing stains. When replacing a toilet, we can recommend models with surfaces designed to resist mineral buildup and discuss water softening options.

    GLV Plumbing provides a warranty on both our workmanship and the installed toilet fixture. The specific terms vary by toilet brand, but we stand behind our installations to ensure your peace of mind regarding leaks or malfunctions.
