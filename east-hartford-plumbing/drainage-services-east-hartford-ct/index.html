    The cost of drainage services in East Hartford, CT varies a lot depending on the type and severity of the issue.     Common signs include slow drains, gurgling sounds from pipes or toilets, unpleasant odors coming from drains, water backing up into sinks or showers, and multiple fixtures draining slowly at once. If you notice any of these, it's time to call a professional.

    Simple downspout extensions are a reasonable DIY project. French drains and dry wells require correct slope, fabric and outlet placement, and mistakes can push water toward the house. Professional installation also handles utility locating and any permits.

    Tree roots are naturally attracted to the moisture and nutrients found inside sewer lines. This is a common issue in many established East Hartford, CT neighborhoods with mature trees.

    While readily available, chemical drain cleaners are generally not recommended. They often contain harsh chemicals that can corrode pipes over time, especially older ones, and can be dangerous if not handled properly. Professional plumbers use safer, more effective methods.

    Minor drain cleaning typically does not require a permit.     For most homes, a preventive drain cleaning every one to two years is sufficient.
