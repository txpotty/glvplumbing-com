    Gas Range and Cooktop Line Installation in East Hartford

    Switching from an electric range to gas is one of the most frequent reasons homeowners call us. Professional-style ranges can need 60,000 BTU or more, so we confirm the branch and main can handle the load. The appliance is then checked for proper burner flame and ignition.

    New Gas Appliance Hookups in East Hartford

    Adding a new gas range, dryer or water heater needs a dedicated gas line. Our plumbers can install new black iron pipe or corrugated stainless steel tubing, known as CSST, from your main gas supply to your new appliance. We make sure of proper pipe sizing for adequate gas flow and perform all necessary connections and pressure tests for a safe, working setup in your East Hartford home.

    Outdoor Kitchen and BBQ Gas Lines in East Hartford

    Built-in grills, side burners and pizza ovens are a popular part of backyard living. We trench from the house to the island and install yellow polyethylene gas pipe with tracer wire, transitioning to steel above grade with an approved riser. Each appliance gets an accessible shutoff valve. We bury the line to code depth and protect it where it crosses landscaping or sprinkler lines.

    Pool and Spa Heater Gas Lines in East Hartford

    Pool heaters are among the largest gas appliances in a home, often rated at 250,000 to 400,000 BTU. They usually sit at the equipment pad far from the meter, so line size and length are critical. We may recommend a larger main line or a meter upgrade from your gas utility. A properly sized line lets the heater reach its rated output and heat the water quickly.
