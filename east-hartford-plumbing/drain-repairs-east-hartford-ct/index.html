    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in East Hartford

    The cost of drain repairs in East Hartford, CT can vary widely, typically ranging from $150 for a simple clog removal to over $3,000 for complex main sewer line repairs. Factors like the type of drain, severity of the issue and access to the pipes all influence the final price. We provide a detailed estimate after a full inspection.

    Repeat clogs usually come from grease buildup, hard water scale, poor pipe slope, a missing vent or a damaged pipe that catches debris. A camera inspection shows which cause you are dealing with so it can be fixed permanently.

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to clear out stubborn blockages, grease buildup, mineral deposits and even tree roots from your pipes. It is commonly used for severe clogs or as a preventive measure to thoroughly clean drain lines, leaving them almost like new.

    The duration of drain repairs depends entirely on the issue. A simple clog can often be cleared in an hour or two. More involved repairs, like replacing a section of a main sewer line, might take a full day or even several days, especially if permits or extensive digging are involved.

    Minor clogs can sometimes be resolved with plungers or enzymatic drain cleaners. However, for stubborn blockages, recurring issues or suspected pipe damage, it is best to call a professional. DIY attempts with harsh chemical cleaners can sometimes damage pipes or hide a larger problem.

    You should call a professional if your DIY efforts with a plunger or basic snake haven't cleared the clog, or if you have recurring clogs, foul odors or multiple slow drains. These issues usually point to a deeper problem that requires specialized tools and expertise to resolve effectively.

    Minor fixture drain repairs typically do not require a permit. Replacing drain stacks, relocating drains or adding new lines often does, depending on the city. We handle permits when they apply.

    A dry trap, a cracked drain fitting, a failed wax ring or a blocked vent can all let sewer gas into a room. We locate the source with a smoke test or visual inspection and fix it.
