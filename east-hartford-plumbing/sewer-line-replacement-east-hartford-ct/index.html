    Clay and Orangeburg Line Replacement in East Hartford, CT

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, and Orangeburg fiber pipe flattens and blisters with age. Both are near the end of their service life in many older properties. When the camera shows multiple failures, replacement is typically the best value. We remove or burst the old line and install modern pipe with a long expected service life. You get a smooth, root-resistant line to the street.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Line Replacement in East Hartford

    For major damage or certain pipe layouts, traditional trenching may be the most effective method. This involves digging a trench to access and replace the damaged sewer line directly. Our team plans the excavation to minimize disruption to your landscape, using the right equipment for clean, precise digging. We make sure of proper grading and backfilling to prevent future issues and restore your property.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in East Hartford

    Pipe bursting replaces the old sewer line by pulling a new pipe through the path of the old one. A bursting head breaks the old pipe outward while fused HDPE pipe follows behind. This typically needs only an entry pit and an exit pit instead of a long trench. It works well under driveways, mature trees and landscaping. The new pipe is seamless, which leaves no joints for roots to enter.

    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in East Hartford

    Many older homes in East Hartford may have sewer lines made from outdated materials like clay or cast iron, which are prone to cracking, corrosion, and root invasion.     Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in East Hartford, CT
