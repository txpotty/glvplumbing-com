    Shower Diverter Repair in East Hartford, CT

    A diverter sends water from the tub spout to the showerhead. When it wears out, water keeps running from the spout and shower pressure drops. We replace diverter spouts, three-handle diverter stems and multi-function diverter valves. This brings back full flow to the showerhead.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in East Hartford

    A malfunctioning showerhead can spray erratically, have reduced pressure, or even leak. Often, mineral deposits from the water supply can clog the nozzles, or internal components can wear out.
