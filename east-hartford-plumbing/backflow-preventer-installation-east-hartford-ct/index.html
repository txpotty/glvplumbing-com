    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in East Hartford, CT

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in East Hartford, CT, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a popular and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers carefully install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also make sure of proper clearance and drainage.
