    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in East Hartford

    The cost of water heater installation in East Hartford, CT can range from $1,200 to $4,000 or more, depending on the type of unit (traditional tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any needed plumbing or electrical upgrades. Tankless units usually have higher installation costs due to more complex venting and utility requirements. We provide a written, upfront quote after assessing your specific needs.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for water heater replacement. We pull permits when required and confirm the install meets code.

    Many tank water heaters last 8 to 12 years, though hard water can shorten that. Regular flushing and anode rod checks help lengthen life.

    A standard tank replacement usually takes two to four hours. Tankless installs or relocations can take most of a day.

    Most standard water heater installations, especially for replacing a similar tank unit, can be completed within 3 to 4 hours. However, a conversion from a tank to a tankless system, or installations requiring major plumbing or electrical modifications, might take a full day or longer. We will provide an estimated timeline during our first assessment.

    New water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. GLV Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work, giving you peace of mind. We will discuss specific warranty details for your chosen unit during the consultation.

    An expansion tank absorbs excess water pressure created as water heats up inside the tank. Many homes in East Hartford, CT, particularly those with closed-loop plumbing systems, are required by code to have an expansion tank to protect the water heater and plumbing system from damage.

    Rusty water, leaks around the base, rumbling noises and running out of hot water quickly are common signs. If your heater is over 10 years old, replacement is often the smart move.
