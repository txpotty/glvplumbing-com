    Understanding Polybutylene Failure in East Hartford

    Polybutylene's built-in weakness stems from its chemical reaction with oxidants like chlorine, commonly used in municipal water treatment. Over time, this reaction causes the pipes and their plastic fittings to become brittle, leading to hairline cracks and eventual rupture. A large share of homes in Connecticut use private wells, which can be acidic or carry iron, manganese, or radon that call for treatment. Hard water can also add stress to pipe that is already breaking down. Understanding this failure mechanism is crucial for diagnosing issues and recommending the most effective and permanent replacement strategy for your home.
