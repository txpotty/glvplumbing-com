    Frequently Asked Questions About Leak Detection in East Hartford

    The cost of leak detection in East Hartford, CT can range from $250 to $800, depending on the complexity and location of the leak. Factors like slab leaks or hard-to-reach pipe leaks may be at the higher end. We provide a clear estimate after our initial assessment.

    Older homes may have galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes, both known for higher leak rates due to corrosion or material breakdown. Copper pipes can develop pinhole leaks over time, while PVC and PEX are generally more reliable but can still leak at fittings or due to installation errors.

    If a gas leak is suspected, immediately evacuate the property and call 911 or your gas provider. Once the area is safe, GLV Plumbing can be called to precisely locate the leak and perform the necessary repairs according to strict safety protocols and codes.

    To prevent future leaks, consider regular plumbing inspections, especially for older homes. Keep water pressure in a safe range, address any signs of corrosion and maintain your plumbing system. For homes with known issues, proactive repiping or pipe upgrades can be a long-term solution.

    Most residential leak detection appointments take one to three hours. Larger homes, commercial buildings or multiple leaks can take longer. We give you an estimate when we schedule.

    Many homeowner's insurance policies cover the cost of repairing sudden and accidental leaks, and sometimes the cost of leak detection itself, especially if it leads to a covered repair. It's best to check your specific policy or contact your insurance provider for details.

    We use acoustic ground microphones, thermal imaging cameras from brands like FLIR, line tracers, moisture meters and pressure test gauges. Each tool helps confirm a different type of leak. Using several together gives reliable results.

    While you can check for obvious leaks, hidden leaks often require professional equipment like acoustic sensors, thermal cameras or tracer gas to locate precisely. A professional plumber will pinpoint the source without unnecessary damage to your home, saving you time and money.
