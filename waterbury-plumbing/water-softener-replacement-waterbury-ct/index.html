    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Waterbury, CT

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and proven solution for hard water in Connecticut. When replacing these units, we carefully disconnect the old brine tank and resin tank, then install the new components.     Clack Water Softener Replacement in Waterbury

    Clack valves are a popular choice for their reliability and customizable settings. If you have an older Clack system failing, GLV Plumbing offers professional Clack water softener replacement in Waterbury.     Salt-Free Conditioner Alternatives in Waterbury, CT

    Some homeowners prefer a salt-free scale reduction system that uses template-assisted crystallization media. These systems do not remove hardness minerals but change them so they are less likely to stick. They require no drain and no salt. We explain the differences plainly, since they work differently than true softeners. They can be a good fit when drain access is limited or sodium is a concern.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Waterbury, CT

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more cost effective than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes better sense.
