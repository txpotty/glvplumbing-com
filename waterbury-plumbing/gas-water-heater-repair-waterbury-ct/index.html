    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Waterbury

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures usually involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent. We read the error codes, test each part and repair the failure. Correct vent slope and termination are verified before we leave.

    Propane Water Heater Repair in Waterbury

    Rural properties often run water heaters on propane rather than natural gas. Propane units use different orifices and regulator pressures, and incorrect conversions cause sooting and poor performance. We confirm the correct parts are installed and repair pilots, valves and burners. We also check tank regulators for correct pressure.

    Burner Assembly and Venting System Repair in Waterbury

    A malfunctioning burner assembly can result in insufficient heating or unusual noises. We closely inspect the burner for blockages, corrosion or wear. We also check the venting system for proper draft and obstructions, which is vital for safe operation and preventing carbon monoxide buildup in your Waterbury home. Proper venting is a critical safety consideration.

    Pilot Light Repair in Waterbury

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most frequent gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and clean or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are completed in about an hour.

    Pilot Light and Ignition System Repair in Waterbury

    A common culprit for no hot water in gas heaters is a faulty pilot light or ignition system. Our technicians are experienced in diagnosing issues like a dirty pilot tube, thermocouple failure or a bad igniter. We carry common replacement parts on our service vehicles to ensure a quick fix and restore your hot water supply promptly.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Waterbury, CT

    A yellow, lazy or noisy flame points to a dirty burner or poor combustion air. We remove and clean the burner, check the orifice size for natural gas or propane and adjust as needed. A clean blue flame heats more quickly and produces less carbon monoxide. We also check for rust flakes falling from the flue.

    Restoring Hot Water for Older Homes in Waterbury

    Many homes in Waterbury have older gas water heater models that require specialized knowledge for repair.     Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in Waterbury

    Strange noises coming from your gas water heater, such as rumbling, popping or whistling, often indicate sediment buildup in the tank or a burner problem. Hard water can speed up sediment accumulation. Our repair service includes flushing the tank and inspecting for sediment, addressing pressure issues or checking other components that might be causing the noise, improving efficiency and quiet operation.
