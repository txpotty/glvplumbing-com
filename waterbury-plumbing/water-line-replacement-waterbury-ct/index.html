    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Line Replacement in Waterbury

    Water line replacement typically costs $2,500 to $6,500 for most homes. Long runs, rock or trenchless methods can increase cost to $7,000 to $12,000.

    We install PEX and Type K copper. Both are durable and code approved for underground use.

    We provide a written labor warranty on the installation. Pipe manufacturers also offer long material warranties, often 25 years for PEX when installed by a licensed plumber.

    A newly installed water line using quality materials like copper or PEX can last 50 to 100 years or even longer. Copper is known for its extreme longevity, while PEX typically has a manufacturer's warranty of 25 years but can last much longer. Regular maintenance and stable ground conditions contribute to a longer lifespan.

    Low pressure, rusty water, wet spots in the yard and high water bills are common signs. Frequent leaks also indicate the line is failing.

    Homeowners are usually responsible for the line from the meter to the house. The utility handles the line on the street side.

    While trenchless water line replacement is an excellent option for minimizing yard disruption, it's not always feasible. It depends on the existing pipe material (it works best with certain types), the extent of the damage (a collapsed pipe may prevent it), and the layout of your property. Our technicians will determine if it's a viable solution for your home.

    Water line repair involves fixing a single, localized issue, like a single small leak. Replacement is necessary when the line has extensive damage, multiple leaks, is severely corroded, or has reached the end of its service life. Our plumbers will assess if a repair is practical or if replacement is the more prudent long-term solution for your Waterbury home.
