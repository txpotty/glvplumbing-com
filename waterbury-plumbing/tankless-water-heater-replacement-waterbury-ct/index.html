    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Waterbury

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by capturing heat from exhaust gases. They vent through low-cost PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Humid summers bring condensation on cold pipes and musty basements, which can rust fittings and encourage mold near plumbing. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth the price.

    Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Waterbury, CT

    If you are tired of running the tap while you wait for hot water, a tankless with a built-in recirculation pump can bring heat to the fixture in seconds. We program schedules so the pump runs only during your morning and evening peaks. That keeps gas use reasonable while saving thousands of gallons of wasted water a year.
